# Landing-page research: 50 SaaS sites (Oct 2026)

Method: cvent.com, glideapps.com, antimetal.com and landingfolio.com were fetched live on 2026-10-14.
The rest are from working knowledge of each site's long-standing layout. Re-check any detail before quoting it publicly.
No content, logos or copy were copied. These are patterns only.

## Patterns that recur (and what Helm takes)
1. **Problem, then solution, then product proof.** Retool, Basecamp, HoneyBook. Helm's hero is a banner message with real app screenshots below it.
2. **"Replace your stack".** ClickUp, Rippling, Notion. Helm gets a tool grid (paper, WhatsApp, Excel, Sheets, calculator, sticky notes, CAD, phone) that collapses into Helm.
3. **The CTA wording changes per section; the destination stays the same.** HubSpot, Cvent.
4. **Honest trust.** Glide-style security band without badges we do not hold. No fake logos or stats; testimonial slots marked as placeholders.
5. **Pricing as its own page.** Three tiers with the middle one highlighted (Pipedrive, Zoho), INR, annual note.
6. **Colour:** a light single accent (emerald) on warm off-white works best for trust-led B2B. The dark-only look (Linear, Vercel) suits dev tools, not Indian event studios.
7. **Motion:** a short looping story with a play/pause control and a reduced-motion static frame (Framer, Arc). Keep it to CSS transforms only.
