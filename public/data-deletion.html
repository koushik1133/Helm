Data Deletion & Export

How to export or delete data in Helm, who to ask, how long it takes, and what the law requires us to keep.

2. Export your data

Workspace owners can export clients, events, quotes, payments and other records at any time from the app (CSV / spreadsheet). On request we provide a full machine-readable export (JSON or CSV) within 30 days. Individuals can ask for a copy of their personal data (access / portability).

3. How to request deletion

  1. Send the request from the email linked to the account, or in-app, stating what you want deleted (one record, your profile, or the whole workspace).
  2. We verify your identity and authority (for a workspace: the owner). For a request through an authorised agent or nominee, we ask for proof of authority.
  3. We acknowledge within 7 days and confirm what will be deleted, what must be kept by law, and when.
  4. We complete the deletion and confirm in writing.

Under the DPDP Rules, if an account is inactive for the prescribed period, we notify the user at least 48 hours before erasing it, unless they sign in or ask us to keep it.

5. What we may keep

  • Invoices, payment references and tax records, for the period tax and accounting law requires.
  • Security and access logs, for the minimum period required by law (including at least 1 year under the DPDP Rules).
  • Information needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or comply with a lawful order.
  • A minimal suppression record (email only) so we do not contact you again.
  • Aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer identify you.

Retained data is kept secure, used only for that purpose and deleted when the period ends.

6. Closing a studio account

When a studio closes its account or a trial expires without a subscription: paid subscriptions stop renewing; the workspace is read-only for 30 days for export; then all workspace content (clients, events, layouts, files, chat) and team member access are deleted as above. Studios are responsible for exporting records they must keep themselves before closure.

7. When Helm is the processor

For data a studio stores about its clients, the studio decides whether to delete it. Studios can delete individual clients and records in the app. When we receive a request directly from a studio's client we forward it to the studio without undue delay and assist it, as set out in our Data Processing Addendum.
