Helm Events — User Manual

Everything you need to run an event business, from first enquiry to final bill.

What is Helm

Helm is a private workspace for your event business. It replaces spreadsheets, chats and paperwork with one connected system that follows every event through its whole life:

Enquiry → Proposal → Quote → Confirm → Plan → Event day → Settle & close

Your data is private to your studio — other businesses using Helm can never see it.

Sign up & your studio

The first person to join creates the studio. Everyone else is invited into it (see Team & roles).

  1. Open the app and click Sign up (or Continue with Google).
  2. You'll be asked to Set up your studio — enter your business name, pick your currency and timezone.
  3. That creates your private workspace, pre-loaded with default pricing, chair types and catalogs you can edit later.
Were you invited by a colleague? Don't create a new studio — open the invite link they sent you and sign in / create your account there. That adds you to their studio with the role they chose.

Team & roles

Add teammates from Control Center → User control. Each person gets a role, and each role can be tuned to see or edit exactly the areas you allow (leads, quotes, inventory, finance, and so on).

  1. Go to Control Center (⚙ in the top nav) → User control tab.
  2. Use Invite by email link — enter their email + role → copy the link → send it to them.
  3. Use the Access control matrix to tick which areas each role can view and edit.

1 · Leads

Every enquiry starts as a lead. Capture it, qualify it on the pipeline, then convert the good ones into events.

  1. Open Leads from the home nav.
  2. Click + New lead and fill in the enquiry (name, phone, event type, date, budget).
  3. Drag the lead card across the pipeline columns (New → Qualified → …) as it progresses.
  4. When it's ready, click Convert → to turn the lead into a quote/event and open its workspace.
Auto-sync: every lead is snapshotted to the CRM archive whenever you create, edit, convert or delete it — so nothing is ever lost, and past clients are always searchable.

2 · Quote & pricing

Price the event and share it with the client.

  1. From the event, open Quote. Set guest count, chairs, catering (in-house or client), and any extras.
  2. Helm shows a live breakdown — rental, catering, service charge, GST, discount and total.
  3. Save a version each time you revise, so you always have a history.
Default rates (chair price, plate price, GST %, service charge) come from Control Center → Pricing. Set these once and every new quote uses them.

3 · Floor plan (builder)

Design the room in 2D/3D — drop chairs, round tables, stage, bars, and more onto a canvas sized to your hall.

  1. Enter the hall length & breadth — the canvas scales to those real measurements with a ruler.
  2. Set your guest count; Helm can auto-fill chairs and tables.
  3. Drag objects from the toolbar onto the floor; move, rotate and duplicate them.
  4. Switch to 3D to preview, and Save the layout back to the event.
Coming improvements: smarter auto-arrangement that spaces tables/chairs to fit the exact hall size, and fully customisable object counts. (In progress.)

4 · Confirm & payment

Lock the event and take the client's approval.

  1. From the event, choose Advance to Confirmed.
  2. Send the client the approval link; they approve by OTP / consent / payment link.
  3. Record the advance payment. (Live card/UPI via Razorpay is available once enabled.)

5 · Plan & resources

Assign tasks, book staff and vendors, reserve inventory, and build the run-sheet.

  • Staff — assign your in-house team to tasks by section (stage, lighting…).
  • Inventory — check equipment out to a person and back in; anything missing is flagged with its value.
  • Vendors — book outside partners for gaps your team can't cover.
  • Run-sheet & budget — a timed schedule and a live cost vs. quote view.

6 · Event day

Run the day from Command: check in guests and staff, tick the run-sheet, and log any issues live.

7 · Settle & close

After the event: tear down and return inventory, invoice and collect the balance, settle vendors, then close with feedback and a clear P&L.

CRM & nurture

CRM keeps every past client and enquiry for reference and follow-up. Nurture can auto-send birthday/anniversary greetings to win repeat business.

Invitation websites

Once an event is confirmed, you can build a public digital invitation website for the client to share with their guests.

  1. On the event, click ✨ Invitation site.
  2. Pick one of the templates (wedding, birthday, corporate, engagement, general).
  3. Fill in the details, add photos and optional background music, preview live.
  4. Click Publish — you get a clean shareable link like helm.events/i/name-xxxx and a Share button on the page.

Control Center

The admin hub (⚙). Set default pricing, chair/plate types and coupons; manage users & roles; and download a GDPR data export of your studio.

FAQ

A teammate can't see something

Check Control Center → Access control — their role may not have view/edit on that area.

The Google button says it isn't enabled

An admin needs to finish the Google OAuth setup in the Supabase dashboard (one-time).

I created a studio by mistake

Ask an admin — teammates should join via an invite link, not by creating a new studio.
