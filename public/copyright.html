1. Our content

2. Reporting infringement

Send a notice to our designated agent at [OWNER TO FILL: COPYRIGHT AGENT NAME, EMAIL, POSTAL ADDRESS] including:

  • Your physical or electronic signature;
  • Identification of the copyrighted work or right claimed to be infringed;
  • The exact location (URL or link) of the material;
  • Your contact details;
  • A statement of good-faith belief that the use is not authorised; and
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and you are authorised to act.

This follows US 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3) (DMCA) and supports notices under India's IT Act s. 79 and IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules 2021 (action within 36 hours of a valid court or government order), the UAE Federal Decree-Law 38/2021 on Copyright, and the EU Digital Services Act Art. 16 notice-and-action mechanism. Our DMCA agent registration number is [OWNER TO FILL: US COPYRIGHT OFFICE DMCA DESIGNATION NO.].

3. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake, send a counter-notice with the elements required by 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3). We may restore the content after 10–14 business days unless the complainant tells us it has started court proceedings. Under the DSA we will give affected users a statement of reasons and access to our internal complaint process.

4. Repeat infringers

We will terminate, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who repeatedly infringe.

5. Other unlawful content

To report other illegal or abusive content, see our Acceptable Use Policy or contact the Grievance Officer on our Grievance page.
