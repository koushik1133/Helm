Acceptable Use Policy

Simple rules that keep Helm safe, fast and trustworthy for every studio.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

On this page 1. Purpose 2. You must not 3. Security research 4. Enforcement 5. Report abuse

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") keeps Helm safe and reliable for every studio. It is part of our Terms of Service. It applies to everyone who uses a Helm workspace, including invited team members and anyone viewing shared links.

2. You must not

  • Break any law, or use Helm for fraud, money laundering or deceptive practices.
  • Store or send content that is unlawful, defamatory, hateful, harassing, sexually exploitative, or that infringes others' intellectual property or privacy.
  • Send unsolicited bulk messages (spam) by email, SMS or WhatsApp, or message people without the consent the law requires.
  • Collect or store personal data without a lawful basis or required notice, or store special-category data, government ID numbers or full payment card numbers in free-text fields.
  • Upload malware, or attempt to probe, scan, test or bypass our security or another customer's workspace, except through our responsible disclosure process.
  • Overload the service, scrape it, or use bots beyond documented APIs and rate limits.
  • Resell, sublicense or white-label Helm without written permission, or build a competing product using it.
  • Impersonate any person or business, or misrepresent your affiliation.

3. Security research

We welcome good-faith reports. Email [ABUSE / SECURITY EMAIL] with details, give us reasonable time to fix the issue, and do not access, change or delete other customers' data.

4. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and remove content, limit features, suspend or terminate accounts, and report unlawful activity to authorities. Where practical we will notify you first and give you a chance to fix the issue, unless urgent action is needed to prevent harm.

5. Report abuse

Report misuse of Helm to [ABUSE / SECURITY EMAIL].
