How email works and how to set it up

For your studio (nothing to set up)

  1. Open Settings → User control (or the Staff page) and invite someone by e-mail.
  2. Helm e-mails the invite within about 2 minutes. If it does not arrive, ask them to check spam, then press Resend email (once every 10 minutes).
  3. The e-mail says which studio and role it is for. Client e-mails show your Sender name; replies go to your Reply-to e-mail (set both above, then press Send test e-mail to me).

WhatsApp

Task assignments, event reminders and payment reminders sent to your crew and clients on WhatsApp.

Pick the country, then type the number (saved in international format, e.g. +91 98765 43210). Test messages go to this number. Only a studio admin can send one, and the number must be the mobile saved in your (or a teammate's) Helm profile.
Choose which messages go out

Only people who can edit Settings can change the number. Settings → Notifications

Step-by-step: connect WhatsApp

A. Meta (about 1–3 days, mostly waiting for verification)

  1. Go to business.facebook.com and create (or open) the company's Meta Business account. Business Settings → Business info → start Business verification (GST certificate or incorporation document).
  2. Go to developers.facebook.com → My Apps → Create app → type Business → link it to that business. Inside the app choose Add product → WhatsApp.
  3. WhatsApp → API Setup → Add phone number. Use a number that is NOT on the normal WhatsApp phone app. Verify it by SMS or call and set the display name.
  4. Copy the Phone number ID (a long number shown under the phone number – not the phone number itself).
  5. Make a permanent token: Business Settings → Users → System users → Add (Admin) → Assign assets (the app and the WhatsApp account, full control) → Generate new token → expiry Never → tick whatsapp_business_messaging and whatsapp_business_management. Copy it once into a password manager. The 24-hour token on the API Setup page will NOT work.

B. Message templates (WhatsApp Manager → Message templates → Create)

Category Utility, language English (code en), exactly ONE variable {{1}}. Helm puts the whole message into {{1}}.

Template nameBody (copy exactly)Used for
helm_task_assignedNew assignment from your studio: {{1}}A crew member is given a task or run-sheet line
helm_event_reminderEvent reminder from your studio: {{1}}"Event in N days" reminders
helm_noticeUpdate from your studio: {{1}}Everything else (payment reminders, follow-ups, notification copies)

Example variable for Meta's review: Stage setup at 4 PM for Rao Wedding. Open your link: https://www.helm.events/work

C. Give the details to Helm

  1. Send these to Helm support (never by e-mail in plain text – use a password-manager share): the Phone number ID, the permanent token, the App secret (App → Settings → Basic) and the WhatsApp Business Account ID.
  2. Helm sets them up on its side and switches WhatsApp on for your studio. You do not need to do anything else here.

D. In Helm

  1. Enter your studio WhatsApp number with country code in the form above (e.g. +91 98765 43210) and press Save number, then Send test WhatsApp. It says Simulated until Helm has switched WhatsApp on, and Live after.
  2. In Settings → Notifications → Automatic client messages & WhatsApp, switch on the messages you want (payment reminders, follow-ups, WhatsApp copies of notifications).
  3. Each crew member opens their profile and ticks Also send my Helm notifications to my WhatsApp.
  4. Test with one number first. Anyone who replies STOP is never messaged again until they reply START.

Online payments (Razorpay)

Clients pay advances and balances by a payment link. The money goes straight into your own Razorpay account.

Checking…

Step-by-step: connect Razorpay
  1. Sign up at dashboard.razorpay.com and finish KYC (PAN, bank account, business documents).
  2. Start in Test mode: Account & Settings → API keys → Generate test key. Keep the Key ID (rzp_test_…) and the Key Secret.
  3. Send the keys to Helm support through a password-manager share. Helm stores them as secrets, deploys the payment functions and gives you a webhook address.
  4. In Razorpay → Webhooks → Add: paste that address, make up a long secret (give the same secret to Helm), tick payment_link.paid.
  5. Helm sends a ₹1 test link. When it shows as paid in Helm, repeat with Live keys.

Until this is on, you can still record payments by hand on each event (cash, UPI, bank transfer).
