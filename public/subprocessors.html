Sub-processors

The service providers that may process personal data on our behalf, and how we tell you before we add a new one.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

On this page 1. What a sub-processor is 2. Current sub-processors 3. Notice of new sub-processors 4. Studio-chosen integrations

1. What a sub-processor is

A sub-processor is a third party that Helm engages to process personal data contained in a studio's workspace (Customer Data) or in our own account records. Each one is bound by a written contract with data protection terms at least as protective as our Data Processing Addendum (GDPR Art. 28(4); UK GDPR Art. 28(4); India DPDP Act s. 8(2); UAE PDPL Art. 23).

3. Notice of new sub-processors

We will update this page and email workspace owners who have subscribed to updates at least 30 days before a new sub-processor starts processing Customer Data. You may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period; if we cannot address the objection, you may terminate the affected service and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees, as set out in the DPA. To subscribe, email [OWNER TO FILL: PRIVACY EMAIL].

4. Studio-chosen integrations

Integrations a studio switches on itself (for example its own Razorpay or WhatsApp account) are engaged by the studio as controller; the provider's own terms apply between the studio and that provider.
