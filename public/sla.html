1. Scope

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") forms part of the Terms of Service and applies to paid subscriptions only. It does not apply to free trials, sandbox or demo workspaces.

2. Availability commitment

3. Exclusions

  • Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead (normally during low-traffic hours, India Standard Time), and emergency security maintenance.
  • Events beyond our reasonable control (force majeure), including failures of the internet or of third-party networks outside our control.
  • Problems caused by your equipment, network, browser, misuse, or breach of the Terms.
  • Optional or beta features, third-party integrations (e.g. WhatsApp, payment gateways) and AI features.
