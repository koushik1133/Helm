4. Free trial

New workspaces may start with an 8-day free trial. No payment card is required to start. In plain words, the free trial works like this:

  • Length: 8 days from sign-up.
  • Region: the trial studio is set up for India, with prices in Indian rupees (₹, INR) and times in India Standard Time (IST).
  • Sample data: the studio starts with ready-made sample data (sample events, leads, inventory, vendors, staff, venues, menus and layouts). Every sample record is marked “Demo”. Sample contacts are not real people.
  • Team: you plus up to 2 teammates can use the trial studio.
  • Adding records: you can add up to 2 new records of each kind (leads, events, inventory items, vendors, staff and venues) in total. Sample records do not count, and deleting a record does not free a slot.
  • Menus: menu prices can be edited; new dishes, menu packages, chair or plate types cannot be added during the trial.
  • Test mode: no real e-mails, WhatsApp or SMS messages are sent to clients during the trial.
  • Paid features: downloads (PDF, CSV and prints), imports and photo renders unlock on a paid plan.
  • After the trial: when the 8 days end, the workspace becomes read-only. You and your teammates can still sign in and look, but nobody can add, change or delete anything until you choose a plan.
  • Keeping your data: trial data is kept for 60 days after the trial ends and is then permanently deleted. We e-mail reminders before that (30, 7 and 1 day before deletion).
  • One trial: one free trial per organisation or e-mail address. Helm may, at its discretion, approve a single extension of up to 7 days.
  • Upgrading: you can choose a paid plan at any time during or after the trial to keep everything and unlock the workspace.

At the end of the trial you must choose a paid plan to keep using Helm. Deletion after the retention period is also described in our Data Deletion policy. We may change or end trial offers for new sign-ups at any time.
