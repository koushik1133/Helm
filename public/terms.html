4. Free trial

New workspaces may start with an 8-day free trial. No payment card is required to start. In plain words, the free trial works like this:

  • Length: 8 days from sign-up.
  • Region: the trial studio is set up for India, with prices in Indian rupees (₹, INR) and times in India Standard Time (IST).
  • Sample data: the studio starts with ready-made sample data: 16 leads (clients), 7 events with quotes, 41 client requirements, 165 inventory items, 16 vendors, 12 staff, 4 venues, 6 menu packages, 145 dishes, 3 layout templates and 3 coupons. Every sample record is marked “Demo” (tagged “Sample”). Sample contacts are not real people.
  • Team: you plus up to 2 teammates can use the trial studio. Open invitations count toward the 2.
  • Adding records: you can create up to 2 new quotes (events) in total. You can add up to 2 new records of each other kind (leads / clients, inventory items, vendors, staff and venues) in total. Sample records, including the 7 sample events, do not count (a sample record renamed without “Demo ·” becomes your own and counts), and deleting a record does not free a slot. You can edit the sample data.
  • Menus: menu prices can be edited (price per plate, chair and plate prices); new dishes, menu packages, chair or plate types cannot be added, renamed or removed during the trial.
  • Included tools: the 2D/3D layout builder with the 3 layout templates, a blank floor or a past event; pricing & coupons; layout rules; roles & access.
  • E-mail to clients: e-mail works during the trial. Approval (quote) links, client booklet and invitation website links and approval codes (OTP) sent by e-mail really reach your clients, up to 30 e-mails a day per studio and 5 a day to one address; sample (demo) events are never e-mailed. You are responsible for e-mailing only clients who expect to hear from you.
  • WhatsApp, SMS and payments: WhatsApp messages, SMS approval codes and online payment links are not sent during the trial and unlock on a paid plan. A client can still confirm a quote in test mode, and payments are recorded by hand.
  • Paid features: downloads (PDF, CSV and prints), imports and photo renders unlock on a paid plan. Studio setup and branding, data export, notification settings, client link auto-expire, your own templates and the activity log are also locked during the trial.
  • After the trial: when the 8 days end, the workspace becomes read-only. You and your teammates can still sign in and look, but nobody can add, change or delete anything until you choose a plan.
  • Reminders: we remind you 3 days before the trial ends, 1 day before, on the last day and 7 days after it ended.
  • Keeping your data: trial data is kept for 60 days after the trial ends and is then permanently deleted. We e-mail reminders before that (30, 7 and 1 day before deletion).
  • One trial: one free trial per organisation or e-mail address. Helm may, at its discretion, approve a single extension of up to 7 days.
  • Upgrading: you can choose a paid plan at any time during or after the trial to keep everything and unlock the workspace. When you upgrade you choose to keep the sample data, remove the sample data and keep your own records, or start completely clean.
  • Agreement: before you enter a trial studio we ask you to read these trial terms and tick “I agree”; we record the date and time of your agreement.

At the end of the trial you must choose a paid plan to keep using Helm. Deletion after the retention period is also described in our Data Deletion policy. We may change or end trial offers for new sign-ups at any time.
