      FAQ

      Questions, answered.

      What exactly is Helm?

      A workspace for event studios, planners and decorators. It holds your leads, venue layouts (2D to exact measurements, plus 3D views and photo renders), quotes, tasks, crew, stock, vendors, payments and profit — in one place.

      How long is the free trial?

      8 days, no card needed. Your trial studio is set up for India (₹, IST) and comes with ready-made sample events, leads, menus, inventory and more, all marked “Demo”. You and up to 2 teammates can use it, e-mail to your clients works (WhatsApp and SMS unlock on a paid plan), and downloads, imports and photo renders unlock on a paid plan. After 8 days the studio becomes read-only; your data is kept for 60 days, so you can choose a plan and keep everything. One free trial per email. See all free-trial rules

      Who is Helm for?

      Event businesses anywhere: wedding planners, decorators, corporate and conference teams, festival organisers and studios running private, cultural and public events — from one person to multi-city teams.

      Does it work in my country and currency?

      Helm lets each studio set its currency, tax lines and time zone, and layouts work in metres or feet. Talk to us about local requirements for Enterprise.

      Do I still need a separate CAD tool?

      For event layouts, no. Helm's builder draws the venue to exact measurements and shows the same plan in 3D, and the items on the plan feed the quote.

      Can clients and crew use it without installing anything?

      Yes. Clients open a link to view the booklet and approve; crew open their own link to see only their tasks. It runs in the browser on laptop and phone.

      Can Helm handle many events at once?

      Yes. Each event keeps its own layout, quote, tasks and payments, and you can see all of a client's events side by side.

      How is my data kept safe?

      Each studio's workspace is kept separate by database rules, connections use HTTPS, access follows the roles you set, and key changes are logged. See our Privacy Policy.

      Are prices inclusive of tax?

      Proposed prices are shown before tax. Checkout shows the exact tax for your billing details.

      Can I change plans later?

      Yes. Your events, layouts and records stay with your workspace when you move between plans.
