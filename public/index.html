FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is HELM?
HELM is a comprehensive event management platform that centralises every aspect of event operations — from planning and budgeting to execution and post-event analytics — in one unified system, replacing scattered spreadsheets and disconnected tools.
Who should use HELM?
Event managers, coordinators, corporate event teams, non-profit organisers, venue managers — anyone responsible for running events. Whether you manage one large annual event or dozens a year, HELM scales to your needs.
How is HELM different from spreadsheets and basic apps?
Unlike spreadsheets, HELM offers real-time collaboration, automatic task escalation, live budget tracking and instant visibility across your whole operation. Unlike basic apps, it combines event management with CRM, marketing and management-level analytics — a complete business solution, not just a task tracker.
Can HELM handle multiple events at once?
Yes. HELM is designed to manage multiple concurrent events, each with its own budget, timeline, vendor network and client records — all within one platform.
How secure is HELM?
HELM is built on security-first architecture. Each organisation's workspace is fully isolated, connections are encrypted, access is role-based, and features are security- and quality-checked before release.
