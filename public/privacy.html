1. Who we are and what this covers

Helm is an event-management platform operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] (company no. [COMPANY REGISTRATION NUMBER]), [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("Helm", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit our website, start a trial, subscribe, or use the Helm app.

Two roles. For account, billing, website and support data, Helm decides why and how data is used, so we are the Data Fiduciary (India DPDP Act) / controller (GDPR, UK GDPR) / business (CCPA). For the client, guest, vendor and event data a studio stores in its workspace, the studio is the Data Fiduciary / controller and Helm acts only as its Data Processor / processor / service provider under our Data Processing Addendum.

If you are a client or guest of a studio that uses Helm, please contact that studio first about your data. We will help them answer you, and if you contact us directly we will pass your request to them.

4. Notice and consent (India DPDP)

Before or at the time we ask for your personal data we tell you what we collect, the purpose, how to withdraw consent, how to exercise your rights and how to complain to the Data Protection Board of India. This notice is available in English and, on request, in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution of India. Where a Consent Manager registered with the Board is used, you may give, manage and withdraw consent through it.

5. Who we share it with

  • Subprocessors that help us run Helm (hosting and database, email delivery, error monitoring, payments, messaging). They act on our instructions under written contracts. The current list is on our Sub-processors page.
  • Your studio: if you are a team member, your studio admin can see your activity in that workspace.
  • At your direction: when you send a quote, invitation, receipt or message to a client, or connect an integration.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law, court order or a lawful government request, or to protect people, our users or Helm.
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or asset sale, under confidentiality and with this policy continuing to apply.

We never sell personal data or rent mailing lists.

6. International transfers

Helm is a global service and data is hosted in [HOSTING REGION(S)]. When personal data moves across borders we use appropriate safeguards: for transfers out of the EEA, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs); for the UK, the International Data Transfer Addendum; and for India, we transfer only to countries not restricted by the Central Government under section 16 of the DPDP Act; for the UAE, to countries with adequate protection or under the safeguards in Articles 22–23 of the PDPL. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

8. Security and breach notification

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, row-level access controls that keep each studio's workspace separate, role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication options, least-privilege staff access, audit logging, backups and regular security testing.

If a personal data breach occurs we will contain it and, as the DPDP Rules require, inform affected people without delay and the Data Protection Board of India, with a detailed report within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Under GDPR / UK GDPR we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required. Where we act as a processor we notify the studio without undue delay so it can meet its own duties.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, and who it has been shared with.
  • Correct, complete or update it.
  • Erase it (subject to legal retention).
  • Data portability: receive it in a common machine-readable format (GDPR / UK GDPR).
  • Object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable (DPDP Act s. 14).
  • Grievance redressal: a response from our Grievance Officer within 90 days; then complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing (we do neither), limit use of sensitive data, and not be discriminated against for exercising rights. You may use an authorised agent.
  • Complain to us (see our Grievance page), then to your local supervisory authority (EU/EEA), the UK Information Commissioner's Office, the UAE Data Office or the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise a right, email [PRIVACY EMAIL] or follow our data request steps. We verify your identity before acting and reply within one month (GDPR), 45 days (CCPA) or the time required by the DPDP Rules, and in any case within 90 days. Requests are free unless clearly unfounded or excessive.

11. Children

Helm is a business tool for adults and is not directed to children. Account holders must be 18 or older. We do not knowingly process data of children (under 18 in India; under 16 or the local age of digital consent elsewhere) except where a studio stores, for example, a child guest's name for an event; the studio must then obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian as the DPDP Act s. 9 requires. We do not track, profile or target advertising at children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact [PRIVACY EMAIL] and we will delete it.

12. Cookies

We use essential cookies and browser storage to sign you in and keep Helm secure, and, only with your consent where required, analytics. Details and controls are in our Cookie Policy. We honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out where applicable.
