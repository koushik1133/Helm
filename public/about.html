✦ About Helm

Building the future of event management.

At Helm, we’ve witnessed firsthand the chaos that can derail an event. That’s why we created a unified platform that simplifies planning, improves collaboration, and delivers complete visibility for event teams.

Our story & mission

Helm was built from a simple belief: event professionals shouldn’t manage events through chaos. Teams deserve one unified platform that automates workflows and provides real-time visibility.

Today, Helm supports conferences, festivals, trade shows, hospitality events, and corporate organizations across multiple industries.
