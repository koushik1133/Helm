Data Processing Addendum

Terms under which Helm processes personal data on behalf of the studios that use it, aligned with DPDP, GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Scope and roles

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service between To be updated ("Helm", "Processor") and the Customer ("Controller"). It applies when Helm processes personal data in Customer Data on the Customer's behalf. The Customer is the controller under GDPR / UK GDPR, the Data Fiduciary under the India DPDP Act 2023, and the business under CCPA/CPRA; Helm is the processor, Data Processor and service provider respectively.

2. Details of processing

Subject matter and durationProviding the Helm platform for the term of the subscription plus the deletion periods in Section 10

3. Processing only on instructions

Helm processes personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions (these Terms, this DPA and the Customer's use of the service), unless required by law, in which case Helm informs the Customer unless legally prohibited. Helm will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction infringes data-protection law. Under CCPA, Helm will not sell or share the data, or use it outside the direct business relationship.

4. Confidentiality

Personnel with access are bound by confidentiality and receive privacy and security training. Access follows least-privilege and is logged.

5. Security measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.
  • Logical tenant isolation enforced by database row-level security.
  • Role-based access, MFA for administrative access, audit logging.
  • Backups, tested restoration, and business-continuity planning.
  • Vulnerability management, dependency monitoring and periodic security testing.
  • Logs retained for at least 1 year as the DPDP Rules require.

6. Subprocessors

The Customer authorises Helm to use the subprocessors listed at helm.events/subprocessors. Helm imposes data-protection terms on each that are at least as protective as this DPA and remains responsible for them. Helm gives at least 30 days' notice of new subprocessors; the Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds, and if we cannot resolve it the Customer may terminate the affected service and receive a pro-rata refund.

7. Assisting with data subject requests

Helm provides in-app tools to access, correct, export and delete data, and will promptly forward any request it receives directly and assist the Customer in responding, including within the DPDP 90-day grievance timeline.

8. Personal data breaches

Helm will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting Customer personal data, so the Customer can meet its 72-hour obligations to the Data Protection Board of India and supervisory authorities. The notice will describe the nature of the breach, likely consequences, data and people affected, and measures taken, with updates as information becomes available.

9. International transfers

Where Helm transfers personal data outside the EEA, UK or India, the parties agree to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 controller-to-processor, and Module 3 where applicable), the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and compliance with any restriction notified under section 16 of the DPDP Act. These are incorporated by reference.

10. Return and deletion

At the end of the service the Customer may export its data for 30 days; Helm then deletes Customer personal data within 60 days and from backups within a further 35 days, unless law requires retention. On request Helm confirms deletion in writing.

11. Audits and assistance

Helm makes available information needed to demonstrate compliance, including security documentation and summaries of third-party assessments, and assists with DPIAs and prior consultations. On-site audits may take place once a year with 30 days' notice, at the Customer's cost, under confidentiality.

12. Order of precedence and contact

If this DPA conflicts with the Terms, this DPA prevails for personal data; the SCCs prevail over both. Contact: To be updated, To be updated. To request a countersigned copy, write to To be updated.
