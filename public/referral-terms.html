Referral Program Terms

1. About this program

These Referral Program Terms ("Referral Terms") add to our Terms of Service. They explain how a Helm customer ("you", the referrer) can invite another event business (the new studio) and how both earn free months of Helm. This is a draft. The program is not live yet, and nothing on this page is an offer until we announce the launch.

In short: share your link or code. When a new studio signs up with it and becomes a paying customer, you both get one month of Helm free.

2. Who can refer

  • Any studio with a Helm workspace in good standing. Studios on the free trial can share their link too. Rewards wait until the studio starts paying (see section 6).
  • Each workspace has one personal link (for example helm.events/r/YOURCODE) and one code. Only the workspace owner or an admin can see and share them.
  • Helm employees, contractors and resellers working with Helm cannot earn referral rewards.

3. Who can be referred

  • A business that has never had a Helm workspace. That includes a past trial in the last 12 months.
  • It must be a separate business from yours. A second workspace for yourself, a branch you own or control, a company in your group, or a family member's business run with you does not count.
  • The new studio must sign up through your link, or type your code at sign-up. If it forgets, our support team can add the code within 14 days of sign-up and before its first payment. After that a code cannot be added.
  • One code per new studio. A studio that uses a partner or campaign code cannot also use a referral code.
  • If a studio opened several referral links, the last link it opened within 60 days counts. A code typed at sign-up beats any link.

4. When a referral counts

  1. The new studio signs up, verifies its e-mail, and makes its first real (non-zero) payment for a paid plan within 60 days of signing up.
  2. We wait 14 days after that payment. If it is not refunded, disputed or charged back, and the studio has not cancelled, the referral counts and both rewards are confirmed.

We may hold a referral for a manual check, for example when our systems see signs that the referrer and the new studio may be the same business. A check usually takes up to 7 working days.

5. The rewards

  • New studio: one free month, in addition to the 8-day free trial. On a monthly plan, the renewal after its first payment is free. On a yearly plan, its renewal date moves one month later. A partner or campaign code may give a different offer, shown on the sign-up page.
  • Referrer: one free month for each referral that counts. It is applied to your next renewal. If you earn several, they cover your next renewals one after another, and normal billing starts again after them. For example: you are in your third paid month, a referral counts, your fourth month is free, and billing starts again in month five.
  • A free month is one month of the plan you are on at that renewal. A free month on an Enterprise plan is worth one month of the Studio plan.
  • Rewards are a discount on Helm. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, cannot be transferred or sold, and cannot be used against past invoices or taxes already charged.

6. How free months are used

  • Monthly plans: when your renewal is due, that month is free: your plan simply continues one more month and you are billed nothing for it.
  • Yearly plans: each free month moves your renewal date one month later. You get no partial refund.
  • Currency: the reward is always "one month", in the currency your studio is billed in (US dollars, or Indian rupees for studios billed in India). It is never converted into cash.
  • Plan changes: an upgrade or downgrade changes the value of free months that are still to come. Free months already used do not change.
  • Referrer not paying yet: if you are still on your trial, earned months are held for up to 6 months and start when you begin paying. If you do not start paying within 6 months, they expire.
  • Referrer cancels: unused months are frozen. They come back if you subscribe again within 6 months. After that they expire.

7. Limits and expiry

  • You can earn up to 12 free months in any 12 months. Referrals above that still help the new studio, but do not earn you more months.
  • An unused free month expires 24 months after you earned it.
  • We may lower or raise these limits for the future (see section 13). Months you have already earned keep the rules they were earned under.

8. Partner and campaign codes

Helm also gives codes to partners, creators and campaigns (for example an expo stall or a festival offer). Each partner code shows its own offer on the sign-up page before you sign up, for example "2 free months after your first payment" or "30% off for 3 months". Partner codes can have an end date and a maximum number of uses, and we may switch off a code that is misused.

Partners who receive any benefit from Helm, such as free months, payment or free access, must clearly disclose this connection every time they promote Helm. In India this follows the ASCI guidelines for influencer advertising: a label such as "Ad", "Collaboration" or "#ad" at the start of the post, not hidden in hashtags. Elsewhere it follows local rules, such as the US FTC Endorsement Guides.

9. How you may share

  • Share with people you know, in your own words, from your own WhatsApp, e-mail or social accounts. You are responsible for what you send.
  • Do not spam. That means no bulk or unsolicited messages, no bought contact lists, no posting in groups where it is not welcome, and no auto-messaging tools.
  • Do not bid on "Helm" or similar words in search ads. Do not make sites, pages or ads that look like Helm's own. Do not promise anything Helm does not offer.
  • Helm does not e-mail or message the people you refer on your behalf. Helm does not ask you for their contact details.

10. Fraud and misuse

To keep the program fair, we check every referral. We look at signals such as matching e-mail addresses or company domains, phone numbers, devices, internet addresses, payment cards or UPI, and tax numbers (GSTIN), and at many sign-ups on one code in a short time. If we reasonably believe a referral breaks these Referral Terms, we may refuse or reverse the reward. That includes self-referral, fake or duplicate accounts, or a refund or chargeback. In serious or repeated cases we may also remove your access to the program or suspend your account under our Terms of Service. If a free month was already used for a referral that is later refunded or charged back, we will not charge you for it, but we may remove your access to the program if it happens again.

11. Privacy

  • When someone opens your link, we remember the code in their browser (local storage, not a tracking cookie) for up to 60 days, so the referral can be credited if they sign up. This is described in our Cookie Policy. We only count that the link was opened; we do not store anything else about them unless they sign up.
  • To spot self-referrals we compare scrambled (hashed) versions of signals such as e-mail, phone, tax number, device and internet address. We never show these to the other studio.
  • When a new studio signs up, we record which code it used, the sign-up date and the payment status, to work out rewards and prevent fraud. We keep this for as long as the reward can be used or reversed, and as required for tax and accounting.
  • As a referrer, you see the status of each studio you referred. You see the studio's name only if it agreed at sign-up. Otherwise you see only its city. You never see its e-mail, its people, its clients or its payments.
  • More detail is in our Privacy Policy. Your rights (access, correction, erasure, grievance) apply to referral data as well.

12. Taxes

Free months are given as a discount on Helm invoices. Taxes such as GST are worked out on the amount actually billed. If a reward has tax effects for your business, you are responsible for them. Helm may change how rewards are given, or ask you for tax details, where the law requires it, for example the rules on business benefits in India.

13. Changes and ending the program

We may change these Referral Terms, or pause or end the program, with at least 30 days' notice on this page and by e-mail to referrers. Referrals that already count, and free months already earned, keep the rules they were earned under. We may change or end a partner code at any time if it is misused.
