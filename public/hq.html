              Google-only sign-in for Helm staff

              Off

              When this is on, everyone with a helm.events address signs in ONLY with their helm.events Google Workspace account. Passwords, magic links and password-reset links stop working for them. Two-step stays required for HQ. Studio customers are not affected.

              Careful. If Google sign-in does not work for you, you are locked out of HQ. HQ people whose address is not on helm.events lose HQ access while this is on. The only way back is the emergency SQL in STAFF-GOOGLE-ONLY-STEPS.md, run in the Supabase SQL editor.

              4. Keep STAFF-GOOGLE-ONLY-STEPS.md open: it has the emergency undo.

                      The rules HQ follows

                      • 1No studio business dataHQ shows studios, their people and what they paid Helm. Never a studio’s clients, quotes, prices, events or messages. For that data Helm only processes it for the studio, so HQ staff see it only if the studio asks (none today).
                      • 2Masked by defaultE-mails, phone numbers, PAN, GSTIN and tax ids show as ka•••@studio.in / +91 ••••••••23. “Show” reveals one value and records who, when and which field — never the value.
                      • 3Only what your role needsEach HQ role sees only its pages; the database refuses everything else. Two-step always, sign-out after 30 minutes idle, sign-ins end after 12 hours, a fresh code for big changes.
                      • 4Never storedPasswords (only hashes, kept by Supabase Auth), card numbers or CVV (Razorpay holds them), Aadhaar numbers.
                      • 5Everything is loggedPage views, changes, reveals and exports are in Security & activity, with who and when.
