2. How to raise a grievance

  • Email the right contact above with your name, studio (if any), what happened and what outcome you want.
  • If you are a client or guest of a studio, contact that studio first; we will forward requests about its workspace to it.
  • We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request about personal data.

4. If you are not satisfied

  • India: Data Protection Board of India (after exhausting our grievance process); consumer commissions / National Consumer Helpline 1915.
  • UAE: UAE Data Office; Dubai DED Consumer Protection (for consumer matters).
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
  • EU/EEA: your local data protection authority.
  • USA: your state Attorney General (and the California Privacy Protection Agency for CA residents).
