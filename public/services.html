What Helm does

Every part of running an event, in one connected platform.

Today it is layouts on paper, guessed distances, bills on WhatsApp and profit worked out by hand. With Helm you design to exact measurements, show the client in 3D, and track every task, person, item and rupee through to the final P&L.

Eight categories

What is included

Each area works on its own, and gets more useful because it shares data with the others.

  • CRM & leads

    Every enquiry captured and followed through, with the full client history in one place.

    • Leads pipeline from enquiry to booking
    • Nurture messages and discovery calls
    • Follow-up reminders so no lead goes cold
    • Client history and archive
    • Resource calendar across events

  • Layout design

    Plan the venue to the centimetre, then show the client exactly how it will look.

    • 2D builder with exact measurements
    • 3D view and photoreal render
    • Layout wizard for a quick first draft
    • 80+ décor items incl. floral arches, mandaps and stages
    • Client booklet from the design

  • Quotes & approvals

    The price updates as you design, and the client approves online.

    • Live pricing while you build
    • Quote versions you can compare
    • Clean, shareable proposals
    • Client e-approval

  • Event operations

    From the plan to teardown, every task, person and item is tracked.

    • Event plan, run sheet and quote flow
    • Crew task app, staff and vendors
    • Inventory with clash warnings across events
    • Event-day command and live issues
    • Teardown checklist

  • Money

    Know what has been paid, what is due and what you actually made.

    • Budget per event
    • Payment milestones and receipts
    • Settlement and closure P&L
    • Business overview behind a privacy PIN

  • Analytics

    See which events and clients make money.

    • Reports with exports
    • Insights on profit per event
    • Trends across your events

  • Team

    Everyone on the same page, with access only to what they need.

    • Team chat
    • Role-based access
    • Audit log of changes

  • Client-facing

    Give clients a polished experience that wins the booking.

    • Client portal
    • Design booklet
    • Online approvals
    • Invitation websites for confirmed events
