Refund & Cancellation Policy

Free 8-day trial, cancel any time, and clear rules for refunds. Prices and refunds are in US dollars.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

On this page 1. Summary 2. 8-day free trial 3. How to cancel 4. Refunds 5. How refunds are paid 6. Disputes and chargebacks 7. Your data after cancellation 8. Contact

1. Summary

Try Helm free for 8 days with no card. Cancel any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. New annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days. All amounts are in US dollars (USD).

This policy forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. 8-day free trial

  • The trial is free and does not require a payment card.
  • You are never charged automatically when the trial ends; you choose whether to subscribe.
  • If you add a card during the trial and pick a plan, billing starts on the date shown at checkout.
  • One trial per business. We may decline repeat trials.

3. How to cancel

  1. Workspace owner signs in and opens Settings → Billing.
  2. Choose Cancel subscription, or email [BILLING EMAIL] from the owner's email address.
  3. We confirm by email. Renewal stops and access continues until the end of the paid period.

Where local consumer law gives you a right to a refund or cooling-off period (for example, in the EU or UK for consumers), that right applies in addition to this policy.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method in USD through [PAYMENT PROCESSOR, e.g. Stripe / Razorpay], usually within 5 to 10 business days after approval. Your bank may take longer, and exchange-rate differences or fees charged by your card issuer are outside our control. Taxes charged on a refunded amount are refunded too, and we issue a credit note where required.

6. Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact [BILLING EMAIL] before raising a chargeback so we can fix the issue quickly. Accounts with an open chargeback may be suspended until it is resolved.

7. Your data after cancellation

Cancelling does not immediately delete your data. You can export it for 30 days after access ends; see Data Deletion & Export.
