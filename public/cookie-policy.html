Cookie Policy

Helm uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work, plus optional analytics with your consent.

1. What cookies and storage are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Helm also uses similar technologies such as local storage and session storage. This policy explains which we use and how you control them. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • Where consent is required we ask before setting non-essential cookies, and you can change your choice at any time from the cookie settings link in the footer.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; strictly necessary storage is needed to sign in.
  • We honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out of any sale or sharing (we do neither) and of non-essential analytics where applicable.

UK users. Since 5 February 2026 the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 allows certain analytics cookies used only to improve our own site, and cookies that adjust appearance or functionality to your preferences, to be set without prior consent provided we tell you and give a simple way to object. We still let you switch analytics off at any time. EU/EEA users. Under the ePrivacy Directive non-essential cookies are set only after your opt-in consent. US users. We honour Global Privacy Control as an opt-out. India. Cookie data that identifies you is personal data under the DPDP Act and handled as in our Privacy Policy.
