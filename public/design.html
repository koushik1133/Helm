What this is for: the Design Studio is the sign-off track for each event's floor plan — 2D draft → internal review → 3D → client review → locked — so everyone knows which version is approved. It doesn't store templates. Reusable floor layouts are Layout templates (also “📐 My templates” in the floor builder, or start a new template); invitation website designs are made per event in the Invitation Studio (event workspace → Invitation).

You don't have access to the Design Studio. Ask an admin to grant the Design area for your role in Settings.
