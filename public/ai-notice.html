2. What we do not do

  • We do not use workspace content to train general-purpose AI models, and our AI providers are contracted not to train on it.
  • We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about individuals solely by automated means (GDPR / UK GDPR Art. 22; UK DUAA 2025 ADM safeguards; CCPA ADMT regulations; Colorado AI Act).
  • Automated reminders, pricing calculations and scheduling are rule-based and always editable by the studio.

3. Labelling and accuracy

AI-generated images are labelled as illustrative and may not match the real venue, décor or quantities. Do not rely on them as a quote or a contractual promise. Under the EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency duties (applying from 2 August 2026) we label synthetic content, and we follow India's IT Rules amendments on labelling synthetically generated information as they apply.
