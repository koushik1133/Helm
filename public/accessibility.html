Accessibility Statement

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use Helm and our website.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

On this page 1. Our commitment 2. What we do 3. Conformance status 4. Feedback and alternative formats

1. Our commitment

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, the standard referenced by the European Accessibility Act (applying from 28 June 2025 via EN 301 549), used as the benchmark under the US Americans with Disabilities Act, and aligned with India's Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act 2016 and GIGW guidelines.

2. What we do

  • Semantic HTML, landmarks, skip links and visible focus states.
  • Keyboard operation of navigation and forms.
  • Colour contrast checked against AA ratios; light and dark themes.
  • Respect for "reduce motion" settings.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Responsive layouts that work at 320 px width and 200% zoom.

3. Conformance status

Partially conformant: some parts do not yet fully conform. Known limitations include the visual 2D/3D layout builder (drag-and-drop canvas), which is hard to operate without a pointer, some data-heavy tables on small screens, and generated PDFs that may not be fully tagged.
