# Post-trial policy (owner answers, 2026-10-18)
1. Trial 8 days. On expiry the studio becomes READ-ONLY automatically.
2. Data kept 60 days after expiry; day 60 permanent deletion.
3. Deletion warning emails: 30, 7 and 1 day before deletion ("Your trial data is scheduled for permanent deletion on DD/MM/YYYY. Upgrade now to retain access and continue using your studio.").
4. On upgrade, customer chooses: (1) keep existing sample data [default], (2) remove sample data and keep own records, (3) start completely clean.
5. Kept sample records stay tagged "Sample"; provide "Delete all sample data" action.
6. Invited members keep READ-ONLY access after expiry; nobody can add/edit/delete until upgrade.
7. One free trial per organisation/email; exception only via admin-approved extension.
8. Extension: one-time, up to 7 days, Super-Admin approval, reason recorded, max one.
9. Trial reminders: 3 days before, 1 day before, on expiry day, 7 days after expiry (upgrade reminder).
10. Expired login: user can enter the studio read-only with a prominent banner: "Your trial has ended. Your data is preserved and available in read-only mode. Upgrade now to continue creating and editing records."
