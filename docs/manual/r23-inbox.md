# Inbox (notifications)

Your **Inbox** collects everything Helm tells you about your events - the same things the 🔔 bell shows you. Each person has their own inbox: what you read, finish or snooze does not change anyone else's.

**Open it:** click the 🔔 bell → **Open inbox**, or press **G** then **I**, or go to `/inbox`.

## Tabs
- **Unread** - what you have not opened or marked read yet.
- **All** - everything still in your inbox (read and unread).
- **Snoozed** - things you put away until later. Each one comes back to Unread at the time you picked (marked "Back from snooze").
- **Done** - things you cleared. Nothing is deleted; **Back to inbox** brings one back.

Use the chips (Finance, Operations, Sales …) to show one kind, **Group by** Event or Date, and **Only this event** on an event's heading.

## What you can do
| Action | How | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Open (marks it read) | click the row | **Enter** |
| Mark read / unread | ◉ button or ⋯ menu | **u** |
| Done (clear it) | ✓ button or ⋯ menu | **e** |
| Snooze - later today (3 hours), tomorrow 9:00 am, next Monday 9:00 am, or a date & time you pick | ⏰ button or ⋯ menu | **s** |
| Select several, then Mark read / Done / Snooze them together | tick the boxes (or the box at the top for all shown) | **x** |
| Move up / down | | **j** / **k** |

Every change shows an **Undo** for a few seconds. **Mark all read** marks everything read. In the bell, the ⋯ menu on a row also has Done and Snooze.

Times are in the studio's time zone (India: IST) and dates are dd/mm/yyyy.

## E-mail about unread items
Click **E-mail: …** at the top of the Inbox and pick:
- **Studio default** (what your admin chose),
- **Daily digest** - one e-mail each morning (8:00 am by default) listing what has been unread for at least an hour,
- **Instant, important only** - a short e-mail a few minutes after a payment, client approval, close request, rejected task or low-stock warning, if you still have not read it,
- **No e-mails**.

Helm never e-mails something you already read, finished or snoozed, and never the same item twice. Free-trial studios do not send these e-mails.

## For admins (Control Center → Notifications → Inbox & e-mail digest)
People with Control Center **edit** rights can set:
- Inbox e-mails are **off** until an admin switches on "E-mail of missed notifications" in Control Center > Automations. Off stops all inbox e-mails for everyone.
- **Default for the team** - Daily digest, Instant important only, or No e-mails (the default). Switching inbox e-mails on in Automations makes it Daily digest.
- **Daily digest at** - the hour (studio time, default 8:00 am) and **Only items unread for at least** (default 1 hour).
- **Opening the bell marks everything read** - off by default: an item counts as read when it is opened or marked.

Who sees which notification is still decided by **Notifications - who hears about what** (per role) and each person's muted types; the inbox follows exactly the same rules as the bell.
