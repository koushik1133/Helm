# Plain words & cleaner pages (R24)

Some pages had names that were hard to follow. They now use plain words, and each money page says in one line what it is for. Nothing about your data changed. Only the names and layout did.

## Logistics page: what the tabs are called now

| Old name | New name | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Permits | **Permits & licences** | Licences, police/fire permissions, insurance, safety sign-offs |
| Comms | **Who tells whom (checklist)** | Who must tell whom what, and by when (client, team, vendors, guests) |
| Logistics | **Transport & deliveries** | Trucks, pickups and deliveries: what moves where, who handles it, by when |
| Payments | **Client payments** | When the client pays us, in parts (advance, balance). Reminders go from here |
| Guests | **Guest count** | How many guests we expect, by group (family, company, VIPs) |

Each tab shows a one-line explanation under its title.

## Event-day page

- **Guests arriving (from Guest count)**: the guest groups you entered in Logistics → Guest count show up here on their own. Tap **＋ In** as guests arrive. **↻ Refresh from Guest count** adds any group you entered later.
- **Arrivals & attendance**: your team and booked vendors are listed as soon as you open the page. **↻ Refresh team & vendors** adds anyone assigned or booked after that. Nobody is added twice.
- **Before doors open (checklist)** (was "Setup & technical checks"): what to check before guests come in, like stage, sound, lights, power and safety exits.
- The automatic lists fill in only for people who can edit the Event-day page. Everyone else sees what is already there.

## Issues & incidents

- **Charge the client for this (extra)** (was "Raise a billable change"): opens Budget so you can add a **change request**, which puts the extra cost on the client's bill. Only people who can see money see this link.

## Budget, Settlement, Closure: what each one is for

- **Budget**: *Plan: what this event should cost and earn.* The top tiles show the **Planned profit**. The "Budget & margin" card below shows only the breakdown, so the profit appears once. If your database doesn't have that card yet, the older breakdown card is shown instead, now titled **How the planned profit adds up**.
- **Settlement**: *Money in and out: collect from the client, pay vendors & staff.* The last tile is now **Still to pay out**: money owed to vendors plus approved staff claims not yet paid. The profit tile was removed from this page. The vendor table says **Vendor** where it used to say "Partner".
- **Closure**: *Final result and close the event.* The **Profit** in the Closure summary is the one final profit number. The P&L breakdown is still below it.

## Nurture

- A short legend sits above the list: **Active** = we keep in touch, **Won** = became a lead again, **Dormant** = paused.
- **Done for this year** (was "Followed up"): press it after you follow up. The next follow-up moves one year ahead.
- **Added from a past client** (was "From lead"): this occasion came from the Birthday/Anniversary saved on the client's lead. Change the date on the lead.

## Event page

- **Event documents (contracts, permits, layouts)** (was "Attachments"): keep the signed contract, permits, venue layout and vendor quotes here. Only your team can open them. The client does not see this list.

## Business and Insights

- **Business**: *Money today: what's owed, collected and coming.*
- **Insights**: *How we're doing over time: trends per event & team.*
- The "Top events by profit" list is no longer on Business when you can open Insights, because Insights already has **Profit per event**. Business shows a link to it instead. If your role can't open Insights, the list stays on Business.

## Dashboard

- **My events & tasks** (was "Upcoming & my tasks" / "My work"): events you are on, with your tasks for each.
- **Quotes pipeline** (was "All quotes" / "Your quotes"): every quote in the studio, from draft to confirmed.

## Staff

- **Day rate (per event day)**: what you pay this person for one day of an event, not per hour. The budget uses it to work out crew wages. The same label is used in Control Center → member profile.

## Who can do what

Nothing changed. Each page keeps its existing access rules.
