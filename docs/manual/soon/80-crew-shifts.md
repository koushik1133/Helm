What is coming:
- Crew mark the days they are free or busy from their phone.
- Post an **open shift** for an event; available crew accept it, first come first served.
- On the event day crew **check in** at the venue, and attendance flows into wages and the closure checklist.

Who: managers and coordinators post shifts; crew see only their own shifts.
