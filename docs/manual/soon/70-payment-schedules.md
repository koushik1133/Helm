What is coming:
- Split the event total into instalments - for example 10% advance, 40% a month before, 50% after the event - with due dates.
- Your client sees the schedule on their page; Helm reminds them before each due date.
- Each payment you record is matched to the next instalment.

Who: roles with **Budget & finance**.
