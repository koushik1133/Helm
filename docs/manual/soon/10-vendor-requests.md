What is coming:
- Send a vendor a request for an event (what you need, date, venue) from the event page.
- The vendor replies with a price and sends an invoice through a private link - no login needed.
- Invoices land on the event's budget as vendor bills, ready to approve and pay.

Who: roles that can edit **Vendors**; bills need **Budget & finance**.
