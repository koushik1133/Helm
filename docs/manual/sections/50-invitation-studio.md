# Invitation Studio: templates, photos and music

Make a small invitation website for a confirmed event and send the link to your client - they share it with their guests.

## Open it
On the event page press **✨ Invitation site** (in the footer, or in **•••**). It appears once the event is **Confirmed** or later.

## Build the invitation
1. **Choose a template** - Eternal (wedding), Promise (engagement), Confetti (birthday), Summit (corporate) or Soirée (any event). Helm picks one from the event type; change it any time.
2. **Event details** - names, headline, date, time, venue name and address, map link, welcome message, hosted by, dress code, reply-by date and contact. Dates are shown as dd/mm/yyyy with the day written out, so there's no mix-up.
3. **Photos** (optional) - **＋ Upload photos** or **Add by URL** (up to 60). Remove a photo with ✕. Cover, Full background and Behind names take one photo each (a new choice moves the old photo to the Gallery). For each photo choose **Where photo N shows**:
   - **Cover** - big photo across the top with the names on it;
   - **Full background** - behind the whole invitation, softly faded. Use **Fade** and **Blur** to keep the text easy to read;
   - **Behind names** - framed behind the names, under the cover;
   - **Gallery** - in the photo gallery;
   - **After a section** - after Details, Schedule or RSVP.
4. **Music** (optional) - pick a song from the **Helm music library (free to use)**, or **Custom link…** for your own MP3 (https link). Press **▶ Preview song** to listen. Guests hear the music only after they tap **Open Invitation**, and can mute it.
5. **Schedule** (optional) - **+ Add schedule item** for each part of the day (time and what happens).

## Preview, publish and send
1. Watch the **Live preview** on the right (on a phone, tap **Preview & publish**).
2. Press **Save draft** while you work, then **Publish**. You see **Published! Share the link below.**
3. Under **Public link** use **Copy link** or **Open site**, or send it from **Send to client** by e-mail or WhatsApp (WhatsApp needs a paid plan and the client's consent).
4. **Unpublish** takes the site down at once.

The link closes by itself 7 days after the event date. Add the event date so this happens.

## Who can do what
| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Build, publish, unpublish, send | Roles that can edit **Quotes & workspace** |
| Preview, copy the link, play songs | Roles that can only view (they see "View only") |
| See the invitation | Anyone with the link, until it closes |
