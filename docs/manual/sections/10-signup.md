# Sign-up, choosing a plan & getting activated

This is how a new studio joins Helm. It is done once, by the person who will be the studio **admin** (usually the owner). Teammates never sign up this way - they join from an invite link (see [Roles & invites](#roles)).

## Where to start
- On the Helm website, every plan card has a main **Pay now** button and a second **Try free for 8 days** button. Enterprise has **Talk to sales**.
- The top of the website has **Start free trial** and **Sign in**.
- The sign-in page is for signing in only. New studios use **New to Helm? Create your studio →** at the bottom.

## The four steps
1. **Your account** - your name, e-mail and a password (type it twice), or **Continue with Google**. Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy**. You can't continue without it.
2. **Verify your e-mail** - open the e-mail from Helm and click the link. If nothing comes, check spam, then press **Resend email** (it waits a little before you can press it again). Google accounts skip this step.
3. **Your studio** - the studio name (2 to 80 letters). Below it you see **Your studio link: helm.events/your-name**. City, team size and the kinds of events you do (Weddings, Receptions, Birthdays, Corporate, Political and more) are optional. Press **Continue →**.
4. **How to start** - pick **Monthly** or **Yearly** (yearly = 2 months free), then one of three buttons on a plan:
   - **Pay now** - creates your studio and takes you to checkout. The studio opens as soon as the payment is confirmed.
   - **Try free for 8 days** - sets up a free trial and opens your dashboard with a short welcome. One free trial per e-mail address.
   - **Contact sales** - creates your studio and opens a short form so our team can call you.

## Checkout
1. Check the plan and Monthly / Yearly.
2. Fill in your **billing details**: studio legal name, GSTIN (optional), country, billing address, city (optional) and state. Helm works out GST from your state and shows it on your invoice.
3. Tick **I agree to the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and the Refund & Cancellation Policy on behalf of my studio**.
4. Press **Pay now**:
   - When online payment is on, you pay through Razorpay. Helm never sees or stores your card.
   - When it is not on yet, the page shows **Pay by UPI or bank transfer** with the UPI ID and bank details. Pay, then press **I've paid — send the payment reference** and type the UTR / reference number.

## Payment pending
After you send the reference, the page says **Payment pending**. Nothing is charged on that page. Helm checks the payment (usually the same working day), activates your plan and e-mails your GST invoice. Until then the studio shows as waiting for its first payment - Helm never says "paid" before the payment is confirmed.

While you wait you can still choose **Start 8-day free trial instead** (only if you haven't had a trial), **Contact sales**, or write to **sales@helm.events**.

## Activation by Helm (HQ)
Helm's own team (HQ) confirms the payment, records it with a GST invoice and switches your plan on in one step. When that happens:
- your trial limits and any read-only mode are lifted straight away;
- if you were on a trial, you choose what happens to the sample data: keep it, remove it, or start clean;
- the next time you sign in, the studio setup wizard opens once so you can add your logo, prices and team.

## Who can do what
| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Create a studio, pick a plan, pay | The person signing up (becomes the studio admin) |
| Change plan or billing details later | Studio admin (Settings → Plan & billing) |
| Confirm a payment and activate a plan | Helm HQ only |
| Join an existing studio | Teammates, from an invite link - never by creating a studio |
