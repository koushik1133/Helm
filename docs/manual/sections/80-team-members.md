# Control Center: the team members table

**Control Center → Users & invites** lists everyone in your studio. Only admins see this section.

## The table
Each row shows **Name** (photo, name, e-mail), **Phone**, **Job title** and department, **Role**, **Profile** (✓ Complete or ✗ Incomplete, and when they joined) and actions. Use **Search name, email, phone, title…** or the **Everyone / Incomplete profiles / Complete profiles** picker to narrow it down. On a phone each person is a card.

## What you can do
1. **Change a role** - pick the new role in the Role column. It saves at once (**Role updated.**). You can't change your own role.
2. **Edit** - change their display name, job title and department. This also updates their Staff record.
3. **Remove** (🗑) - **Delete account** removes their login from your studio. You can't remove yourself. Their past work on events stays.
4. **Add user** - type an e-mail and a role. A one-time temporary password is shown once; share it and they set their own at first sign-in.

## Invite by e-mail link
1. In **Invite by email link** type the e-mail, pick a role and press **Create invite link**.
2. Helm e-mails the invitation (you can also copy the link). It works for 7 days.
3. **Pending invites** lists every open invite with **Copy link**, **Resend email** and **Revoke**.

The seat meter shows **X of Y seats used** for your plan - open invites count as seats. When all seats are used, the add buttons switch off until you free a seat or move to a bigger plan.
