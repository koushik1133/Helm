# Profit and margin: one number everywhere (staff wages included)

Profit on an event is now worked out the same way on every page, and it includes what you pay your staff and crew.

## How profit is worked out
**Profit = revenue without GST − all costs of the event.** Costs include vendors, food, decor, rentals, logistics, petty cash and staff expenses, damages, change orders, contingency **and staff & crew wages**.

Wages come from the crew on the event, at each person's day rate:
- **estimate** while the task is not yet accepted;
- **agreed** once they accept;
- **actual** when they are marked **arrived** on the event day (0 if marked no-show);
- rejected or cancelled tasks don't count.

So 7 people on the event = 7 wage lines. GST you can claim back on vendor bills is not a cost, and refunds come off revenue without their GST.

> If your budget already has a typed **Staff** line, tick **Replaced by crew wages** so the same people aren't counted twice. Helm warns you until you do.

## Where you see it
- **Budget** - the **Budget & margin** card (planned, expected and actual-so-far margin) and the **Profit & loss** section.
- **Closure** - the same card, plus the **Profit** tile in the Closure summary.
- **Insights** - profit per event, event costs & profit, margin trend and the money tiles all show the same margin.
- **Business** - studio-wide profit & payables.

Events closed before this change keep the numbers they were closed with, marked **Recorded numbers (legacy)**.
