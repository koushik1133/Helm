# Using Helm on your phone

Helm works in your phone's browser - no app to install. On a phone (screens narrower than a tablet) you get:

## The bottom bar
Five buttons at the bottom of every studio page:
- **Home** - your dashboard;
- **Search** - find any client, quote, event or page;
- **+** - **Create**: only the things your role can add, such as New lead, New quote / event, Add staff, Add stock item, Add vendor;
- **Alerts** - your notifications, with a red count when something is new;
- **Profile** - your account, Account settings and sign out.

The bar hides while the keyboard is open so it never covers what you type. Clients, guests and the approval / invitation pages never see it.

## Other phone improvements
- Bigger buttons in the top bar, easy to tap with a thumb.
- On the event page the current stage sits in the middle between the ‹ › arrows.
- Next to a date box Helm shows the date written out, e.g. "Thu, 29 Oct 2026", so dd/mm vs mm/dd is never a doubt.
- The **All quotes** list puts the name on its own line with a short status such as "Awaiting approval".
- **Complete your profile** only shows on the dashboard, so it never covers chat, menus or the Share pop-up.
- In the Builder the zoom buttons stay on screen and the floor is centred.

> Tip: on Android Chrome or iPhone Safari use **Add to Home screen** to open Helm like an app.
