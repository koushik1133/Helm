# Covering an event: stock, people, vendors, budget

Once an event is confirmed, open it and click the **Resources** stage. You will see four numbered cards. Work through them in order:

1. **Inventory**: reserve our own stock for this event (chairs, lights, speakers...).
2. **Tasks & staff**: assign our people to the event's tasks.
3. **Resource plan**: see what is still missing, and get it from vendors.
4. **Budget & margin**: check the costs and the margin.

Each of these pages also says which step it is and links to the next one.

## The Resource plan

The table has five columns: **Need · Qty · We have · Missing · Action**.

- **We have** counts what is really set aside for this event: our people **assigned**, or our stock **reserved**.
- **Missing** shows how many are still not arranged. When everything is arranged it shows **✓ Covered**.

### Adding needs
- Click **Pull from discovery requirements** to copy the client's requirements in one go. Helm guesses the type: Catering, Security and Valet become **Staff**; Stage & lighting, Sound, LED and similar become **Our stock** when you own a matching item; everything else is **Other**.
- You can also add a need by hand at the bottom of the table.
- To change a need's type, use the small **Staff / Our stock / Other** box on its row. For **Our stock**, pick the item in the box next to it.

### Covering a need
- **Assign person** (Staff needs): tick the people from your team. Helm shows who is free that day and who is a good match. Each person gets a task in Tasks & staff.
- **Reserve N** (Our stock needs): reserves the stock that is free on the event day. If none is free, the row says so. Get the rest from a vendor.
- **Assign vendor**: choose the vendor, then either:
  - **Send request to vendor**: the vendor gets a link to accept or decline (no sign-in). Enter the date, time, place and agreed amount. After sending you can copy the link or open it in WhatsApp. The answer shows on the vendor's row as *Awaiting reply*, *Accepted* or *Declined*. You can also follow it under Vendors → Requests & invoices.
  - **Already agreed**: note the vendor, the cost and the advance, if you agreed by phone or in person.
- **⋯ → Client/venue arranges it**: use this when the client or the venue brings it, so there is nothing for us to book. **Undo** puts it back to missing.

If a vendor booking for a need is cancelled, the need shows as missing again.

### Vendors for this event
This section lists every vendor on the event with the cost, the advance, the status and the request answer. **Send request** sends a request to a vendor you noted earlier.

## Who can do what
- **View** the plan: anyone whose role can open Resources.
- **Change needs**: roles that can edit Resources.
- **Reserve stock**: roles that can also edit Inventory.
- **Assign person**: roles that can also edit Staff (the person needs a phone number in Staff).
- **Send request to vendor**: roles that can also edit Vendors.
- **Remove** a need or a vendor: roles with delete rights.
