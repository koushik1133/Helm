1 · What is Helm

Helm is a private workspace for your event business. It replaces spreadsheets, chats and paperwork with one connected system that follows every event through its whole life. The quote is the event: you never retype anything as it moves forward.

Lead → Discovery → Proposal → Quote → Confirmed → Planning → Resources → Ready → Event Day → Settlement → Closed

Your data is private to your studio — other businesses using Helm can never see it. Each teammate sees only the parts their role allows (see Roles & invites).

2 · Sign-up & free trial

There are two ways in:

  • Try free for 8 days — a trial studio, no card needed. It comes ready-made with sample data so you can try everything straight away.
  • Pay now — your own studio on a paid plan; it opens as soon as the payment is confirmed. Or Contact sales and we call you.

Were you invited by a colleague? Don't sign up on your own — open the invite e-mail or link they sent and join their studio (see Roles & invites).

Signing up

  1. On the Helm website press Start free trial or a plan's Try free for 8 days (the sign-in page has New to Helm? Create your studio →).
  2. Enter your name, e-mail and a password: at least 12 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol (or use Continue with Google). Tick I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
  3. Open the confirmation e-mail and click the link. If it doesn't arrive, check spam or use Resend email.
  4. Name your studio (this also makes your studio link), then choose Try free for 8 days. Helm opens your trial studio with a short welcome tour.

The whole sign-up, the plan prices, checkout and how a paid studio is activated are in Sign-up, choosing a plan & getting activated.

Sign-up, choosing a plan & getting activated

This is how a new studio joins Helm. It is done once, by the person who will be the studio admin (usually the owner). Teammates never sign up this way — they join from an invite link (see Roles & invites).

Where to start

  • On the Helm website, every plan card has a main Pay now button and a second Try free for 8 days button. Enterprise has Talk to sales.
  • The top of the website has Start free trial and Sign in.
  • The sign-in page is for signing in only. New studios use New to Helm? Create your studio → at the bottom.

The four steps

  1. Your account — your name, e-mail and a password (type it twice), or Continue with Google. Tick I agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. You can't continue without it.
  2. Verify your e-mail — open the e-mail from Helm and click the link. If nothing comes, check spam, then press Resend email (it waits a little before you can press it again). Google accounts skip this step.
  3. Your studio — the studio name (2 to 80 letters). Below it you see Your studio link: helm.events/your-name. City, team size and the kinds of events you do (Weddings, Receptions, Birthdays, Corporate, Political and more) are optional. Press Continue →.
  4. How to start — pick Monthly or Yearly (yearly = 2 months free), then one of three buttons on a plan:
    • Pay now — creates your studio and takes you to checkout. The studio opens as soon as the payment is confirmed.
    • Try free for 8 days — sets up a free trial and opens your dashboard with a short welcome. One free trial per e-mail address.
    • Contact sales — creates your studio and opens a short form so our team can call you.

Checkout

After you send the reference, the page says Payment pending. Nothing is charged on that page. Helm checks the payment (usually the same working day), activates your plan and e-mails your GST invoice. While you wait you can still choose Start 8-day free trial instead (only if you haven't had a trial), Contact sales, or write to sales@helm.events.

Activation by Helm (HQ)

Helm's own team (HQ) confirms the payment, records it with a GST invoice and switches your plan on in one step. When that happens:

  • your trial limits and any read-only mode are lifted straight away;
  • if you were on a trial, you choose what happens to the sample data: keep it, remove it, or start clean;
  • the next time you sign in, the studio setup wizard opens once so you can add your logo, prices and team.

Two-step verification (sign-in code from your phone)

Two-step verification means a stolen password alone can't open your account. After your password (or Google) Helm also asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. It is optional for every role, and strongly suggested for admins.

Two-step is not shown during the free trial. It appears in Account settings once your studio is on a paid plan. (If you had already turned it on, you still see it so you can turn it off.)

Turn it on

  1. Open your profile menu (your initials, top right — on a phone, Profile in the bottom bar) → Account settings.
  2. Find the Two-step verification card. It shows Off.
  3. Scan this QR code with an authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or 1Password. If the app asks, choose "time-based".
    • Can't scan? Press Copy and type the key into the app instead. On a phone you can tap Open in authenticator app.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and press Verify & turn on. Codes change every 30 seconds — type the newest one.
  5. You see Two-step verification is on. From now on Helm asks for a code each time you sign in.

Signing in with two-step

  1. Sign in with your e-mail and password (or Google) as usual.
  2. On the Two-step verification screen, open your authenticator app and type the 6-digit code for Helm.
  3. Press Verify →. No page opens until the code is right.

After 5 wrong codes the screen locks for 15 minutes.

Turn it off

In Account settings → Two-step verification press Turn off two-step. Helm asks for a current code first, so only you can switch it off.

Lost your phone?

There are no backup codes. Write to security@helm.events from your account's e-mail address. Helm support confirms who you are, then resets your two-step. Account settings then shows Helm support reset your two-step verification on (date). Set it up again at your next sign-in.

When your studio makes it compulsory

If your studio requires two-step for admins, an admin who hasn't set it up sees Set up two-step verification at sign-in and can't skip it. Free-trial studios are never forced.

Filter by event date

Next to All types there is a date filter (hover text "Filter by event date"). Pick Any date, Today, Tomorrow, This week (Monday to Sunday), This month, Next month, Next 30 days or Past dates. It uses the date of the event, not the date the quote was last changed.

Search

  • Type a quote code, a client name or a title in Search code, client, title….
  • Smart search: type a short phrase and press Enter. Helm sets the tab, type and date for you and shows what it picked, e.g. Showing: Cancelled · Wedding · This month. Examples:
    • cancelled weddings this month
    • confirmed next month
    • today
  • Smart search understands tab words (cancelled, attention, overdue, confirmed, sent, archived, deleted, in progress, active), date words (today, tomorrow, this week, this month, next month, next 30 days, past) and your event types. If a phrase has any other words, Helm does a normal search instead.
  • Search does not look inside phone numbers, e-mails or amounts — open the client from Clients for that.

Saved views

A view remembers the tab, the type, the date filter and the search text — so "This month's weddings" is one click away.

  1. Set the tab and filters you want.
  2. In the Views: row above the list, press + Save view.
  3. Type a name (up to 60 letters) and press Save view. Admins are also asked Share with studio or Just me.
  4. Your view appears as a chip. Click it any time.

On your own views, the ⋯ menu has Update with current filters, Rename, Set as default (★ — it opens first), Share with studio / Stop sharing (admins), and Delete. Deleting a view never deletes any quote. Views marked · studio were shared by an admin with everyone.

See when your client opens their link

Helm shows you when a client opens the private link you sent them: the client page (proposal), the quotation, the booklet, and the moment they start approving.

Where you see it

  • Quotes list: under the event name, a small chip like "👁 Viewed 3× · last 2h ago". It turns orange with "not approved" when the client has looked but not approved for a while.
  • Event workspace: the same chip next to the status at the top.
  • Quote flow, step 9 (Share with client & approval): the chip plus one line ("First opened 17/10/2026, 4:05 pm · last looked at: quotation"). Before the first open it says "Not opened yet".
  • Bell 🔔: "Rahul opened the proposal" the first time the client opens any page.

Click the chip to open the timeline: each visit with the date and time, the device (Mobile / Tablet / Desktop, Android / iOS, browser) and the pages opened, for example "Proposal → Quotation → Started approving". Pages opened on one device within 30 minutes count as one visit.

What is never counted

  • You and your team, when you are signed in to Helm.
  • The Preview buttons (approval link and booklet), even in a private window.
  • Link previews made by WhatsApp, e-mail apps and search engines.

"Viewed but not approved"

If the client opened their link 2 days ago (you can change this) and still hasn't approved:

  1. The quote's owner (the event manager, otherwise the person who made the quote) and the admins get a bell reminder.
  2. The quote shows in the ⚠ Attention tab on Quotes and under Needs attention on the dashboard.
  3. Open it and tap Send a nudge by e-mail (or Nudge on WhatsApp). Helm sends your studio's own follow-up message to the client's e-mail / WhatsApp saved on the event. Or tap Dismiss if you have already spoken to them.

Notes:

  • A nudge is queued and goes out with Helm's scheduled messages. In a free trial or on a demo event it is recorded but not sent.
  • WhatsApp needs the client's mobile number, their WhatsApp opt-in, and your studio's WhatsApp number (Control Center → Notifications).
  • Up to 3 nudges per client per day. A new reminder only comes after the client opens the link again.
  • When the client approves, the reminder disappears by itself.

Step 9 · Share with client & approval

In the quote flow, step 9 sends your client one private link with the event booklet, the quotation and an Approve button.

  1. Make sure the client's e-mail or phone is filled in at step 1 (Helm asks for it if not).
  2. Keep Ask the client to approve ticked. (Untick it to share the booklet only.)
  3. Press Send for approval →. The page shows Approval link ready.
  4. Send the link the way you like:
    • Copy link and paste it anywhere;
    • WhatsApp (manual) or Email (manual) — opens your own WhatsApp / mail app with the message ready;
    • Send by email — Helm e-mails the client;
    • Send on WhatsApp — through the Helm Events number. Tick Client agreed to receive WhatsApp updates first.
  5. Optional — open Booklet link & settings to choose what the client sees (Floor plan, 3D view, Quotation, Payments, Terms), which quote versions to show, a note, and how long the link works (7 to 180 days, 30 by default). Revoke link stops an old link at once.
Your internal costs, margins and notes are never shown to the client.

You can also press Share with client → in the right-hand rail of the flow — it opens the same controls in a pop-up.

What the client does

  1. Opens the link and sees their event, the quotation with the price breakdown, and the terms.
  2. Ticks I have read and agree to the Terms & Conditions above, types their name and mobile number.
  3. Gets a one-time code by e-mail, WhatsApp or SMS and types it in.
  4. Presses ✓ Confirm & approve. They see Approved & consented.

If the price changes after approval, the client is asked to approve again. In the free trial the client page runs in Trial test mode — nothing is sent and you confirm with Test mode - confirm.

After approval

  • Step 9 shows Approved ✓ with the date, and becomes Share booklet (view-only from now on).
  • Open quote and event pages show a short message Client approved: with the quote code — within about 15 seconds.

Step 10 · Advance payment & booking

Approval is not yet a booking. In step 10 enter Advance % (your studio's default, for example 10%) or Advance amount (₹), pick Cash / offline or Online link, and press Record advance payment. Only then is the event confirmed.

Quote validity ("Valid until")

Every new quote has a Valid until date. It tells your client how long the price holds, reminds them before it ends, and stops an old price from being approved by mistake.

What your client sees

  • On their quote (approval page) and their event booklet: "This quote is valid until 03/11/2026." In the last 3 days it also says how many days are left.
  • After that date: "This quote has expired - ask <your studio> for an updated one." The Approve steps are replaced by a Request a new quote button. Pressing it puts a note in your bell (once a day at most).
  • Once they have approved, or the quote is cancelled, nothing about validity is shown any more.

What your team sees

  • Quote flow, step 9 (Share with client & approval) and the event workspace: "Valid until 03/11/2026 · Expires in 14 days".
    • Extend +14 days — one click. It adds the studio's default number of days (from today if the quote has already expired).
    • Change date — pick any date from today up to one year ahead (only if your studio allows custom dates; admins always can).
    • History — every change, who made it and when.
  • Quotes list: a small pill — grey "Expires in 10 days", amber "Expires in 2 days", red "Expired yesterday". The Attention tab also lists quotes that expire soon or have just expired.
  • Bell: "Quote A-1234 expires in 3 days" (and 1 day), "Quote A-1234 expired — extend it or send an updated quote", and "The client asked for an updated quote". Only people who can see quotes get these.

When the date is set

  • A new quote gets today + the default (14 days).
  • When you send the approval link for the first time, an automatic date is counted again from that day, so the client always gets the full time. A date someone picked by hand is never moved.
  • Quotes made before this feature have no date and never expire. An admin can give them one (see below).

Control Center → Client links & packages → Quote validity

Who can change it: studio admins, or anyone whose role can edit Control Center.

  • Give new quotes a valid-until date (on by default). Off = no dates anywhere, nothing expires or locks.
  • Quotes are valid for (days): 1 to 365 (default 14).
  • Remind before it ends (days): e.g. "3, 1" (default). 0 means on the last day. Up to 5 values.
  • An expired quote can't be approved by the client (on by default). The server refuses the approval even if someone keeps an old page open.
  • Staff may pick a custom date on a quote (on by default). Off = only admins pick dates; everyone who can edit quotes can still press Extend.
  • Quote expiry alert (bell for the team) and Quote expiry reminder (e-mail to the client) are off until you switch them on in Control Center > Automations. Client e-mails are only sent once client e-mail is switched on for Helm; until then they wait in the queue. Free-trial and demo studios never e-mail clients.
  • Apply to open quotes... (admins only): gives every open, never-approved quote that has no date (and whose event is not in the past) today + the default. It shows how many quotes first and asks before changing anything. Quotes that already have a date are never changed.

Invitation Studio: templates, photos and music

Payment schedules

Helm splits what the client pays into a few payments (for example 30% now, 50% before the event, 20% on the day), reminds the client before each one is due, and marks each payment as paid when the money comes in.

What happens on its own

  • When the client approves a quote, Helm adds your default payment schedule to the event. It only does this when the event has no payments planned yet. A schedule that is already there is never changed.
  • The amounts come from the quote total, including GST and any approved change orders. They are rounded to whole rupees, and the last payment takes the small difference so the schedule always adds up to the total.
  • Reminders to the client (off until you switch on "Payment reminders" in Control Center > Automations): 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 3 days after if it is still unpaid. Each reminder includes your UPI ID, your payment note and the online payment link, if one is open.
  • When a payment is overdue (once "Overdue payment alert" is switched on in Automations), the team gets one bell notice ("Client payment overdue"). Admins and roles that can see Finance get it.
  • When you record a payment, every planned payment it fully covers is marked Paid. A part payment shows as "Part paid" with the amount still left.

Set it up (Control Center > Payment schedules)

  1. Pick the Default schedule. Built in: 30 / 50 / 20 (the default), 50 / 50, and 100% upfront.
  2. To make your own, click + New schedule. Add 1 to 10 payments. Each payment has a name, an amount (a % of the total, a fixed amount, or "Balance" = whatever is left) and a due date (days after approval, or days before / after the event). An example on ₹1,00,000 shows as you type. Click Add schedule.
  3. Enter your UPI ID and a short payment note (for example bank details). Clients see these on their page and in reminders.
  4. Payment schedule on approval (on) and Overdue payment alert (off) are switched in Control Center > Automations.
  5. Click Save payment settings.
  • You change reminder timing and wording in Reminders & client messages.
  • Archiving a schedule hides it. Events that already use it keep their payments.

On an event

  • The Payment schedule card shows on the quote's payment step, the event workspace and Budget. Each payment shows as Paid, Part paid, Due or Overdue, with Total / Paid / Outstanding.
  • If an event has no schedule, pick a template, check the preview and click Add this schedule.
  • Pause automatic reminders for this event stops automatic reminders for that one event. "Send reminder now" in Logistics > Payments still works.
  • To change or remove a payment, go to Logistics > Payments.

The client's page

The client sees "Payment schedule" with each payment marked paid, part paid, due or overdue, plus a "How to pay" box with your UPI ID, note and online link.

Profit and margin: one number everywhere (staff wages included)

Profit on an event is now worked out the same way on every page, and it includes what you pay your staff and crew.

How profit is worked out

Profit = revenue without GST − all costs of the event. Costs include vendors, food, decor, rentals, logistics, petty cash and staff expenses, damages, change orders, contingency and staff & crew wages.

Wages come from the crew on the event, at each person's day rate:

  • estimate while the task is not yet accepted;
  • agreed once they accept;
  • actual when they are marked arrived on the event day (0 if marked no-show);
  • rejected or cancelled tasks don't count.

So 7 people on the event = 7 wage lines. GST you can claim back on vendor bills is not a cost, and refunds come off revenue without their GST.

If your budget already has a typed Staff line, tick Replaced by crew wages so the same people aren't counted twice. Helm warns you until you do.

Where you see it

  • Budget — the Budget & margin card (planned, expected and actual-so-far margin) and the Profit & loss section.
  • Closure — the same card, plus the Profit tile in the Closure summary.
  • Insights — profit per event, event costs & profit, margin trend and the money tiles all show the same margin.
  • Business — studio-wide profit & payables.

Events closed before this change keep the numbers they were closed with, marked Recorded numbers (legacy).

Stock for an event: Reserved → Out → Back

Every item you use for an event goes through the same steps, and Inventory, Teardown & returns, Event-day command and Closure all use the same words.

WordWhat it means
ReservedHeld for this event. Nobody has taken it yet.

BackIt came back fine.
MissingIt did not come back. It comes off what you own.

If you reserved something and nobody ever took it out, there is nothing to return. Teardown shows it as "Reserved — never taken out, nothing to return", and Helm releases it for you after the event day.

Before the event: reserve

  1. Open the event and go to Inventory.
  2. Under Stock for this event, pick the item and how many, then press Reserve.
  3. If you don't need it any more, press Release. The stock is free again. Nothing gets deleted.

When stock leaves the store: give it out

  • In Inventory: in the event's row press Give out, choose who it goes to (Given to) and press Give out. You can also use the Out & back tab.
  • On the event day (Event-day command → Live stock requests):
    1. Type in the search box to find the item. The list scrolls and works on a phone. If you don't keep it in stock, pick Use "…" — not from stock.
    2. Enter how many and press ＋ Ask.
    3. Press Give out on the request, choose the person and press Give out. This takes the stock out of Inventory, so the numbers match everywhere. If there isn't enough free, Helm says how many are free and offers to give out just those.
    4. Not needed closes the request. Sourced is for shortages you are renting or buying.

When it comes back: mark it back

  1. Go to Teardown & returns, the event's Inventory page, or the Out & back tab, and press Mark back.
  2. Enter Came back OK and Damaged. Whatever you don't count is Missing.
  3. Confirm. Missing and damaged items come off your stock, once only.
  4. When everything given out for that item is back, Helm releases the item's reservation automatically.

History of an item

In Inventory → Stock items, press History on any item. You'll see every time it went out: which event, who it was given to, when it went out and when it came back. If it's still out, it shows Not back yet.

Closing the event

In the Closure checklist, All stock back only stops you closing when something went Out and hasn't come Back. Stock that was reserved but never taken out doesn't stop you. Missing or damaged stock shows as a warning.

FAQ

I didn't get the confirmation e-mail

Check spam, then use Resend confirmation link on the sign-in page.

My password is refused

It needs 12+ characters with an uppercase and a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol. Repeated wrong attempts lock the account for a short while.

A teammate can't see something

Their role doesn't have that area. An admin can allow it in Control Center → Roles & access.

Operations staff can see prices

Remove finance from their role in the access matrix.

The total differs from my calculator

Tax is applied after the discount and rounded to whole currency units. The server's total is the correct one.

“Payment exceeds amount due”

Payments can't go above the confirmed total plus approved changes. Add a change order first.

Stock shows a shortage

Another event on the same date has reserved it. Check the calendar, then cover the gap with a vendor.

A client link has expired

Send a fresh link from the event. Link windows are set in Control Center → Client links & packages.

I entered a wrong vendor bill

Void it with a reason and add the correct one.

I'm showing my screen to a client

Click the eye in the top bar to hide every amount; showing them again needs the studio PIN.

Where is Control Center / Insights?

In the sidebar under Admin (on a phone: More). The profile menu only holds your own account.

I created a studio by mistake

Teammates should join through an invite link, not by creating a studio. Ask your admin to invite you.
