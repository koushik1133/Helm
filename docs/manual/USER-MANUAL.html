1 · What is Helm

Helm is a private workspace for your event business. It replaces spreadsheets, chats and paperwork with one connected system that follows every event through its whole life. The quote is the event: you never retype anything as it moves forward.

Lead → Discovery → Proposal → Quote → Confirmed → Planning → Resources → Ready → Event Day → Settlement → Closed

Your data is private to your studio — other businesses using Helm can never see it. Each teammate sees only the parts their role allows (see Roles & invites).

2 · Sign-up & free trial

There are two ways in:

  • Start free trial — an 8-day trial studio, no card needed. It comes ready-made with sample data so you can try everything straight away.
  • Create studio — your own empty studio on a paid plan (see Plans & billing).

Were you invited by a colleague? Don't sign up on your own — open the invite e-mail or link they sent and join their studio (see Roles & invites).

Signing up

  1. On the sign-in page choose Start free trial (or Continue with Google).
  2. Enter your e-mail and a password: at least 12 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol. The list under the field ticks each rule as you type.
  3. Read the trial rules, tick I agree (free-trial terms, Terms of Service and Privacy Policy) and continue.
  4. Open the confirmation e-mail and click the link. If it doesn't arrive, check spam or use Resend confirmation link.
  5. Complete your profile (full name and mobile number). Helm then opens your trial studio with a short welcome tour.

3 · Getting around

The sidebar (left)

  • Workspace — Dashboard, Quotes, Leads, Calendar, Chat.
  • Manage — CRM archive, Nurture, Staff, Inventory, Vendors (each only if your role has it).
  • Admin — Business, Control Center, Insights, Reports, Audit log, Studio setup (by role; some are not part of the free trial).
  • Pin — the small pin button at the top keeps the sidebar open with labels. Unpinned, it tucks away to icons and opens when you hover.
  • Pinned pages — star a page to keep it at the top of the sidebar.
  • At the bottom: Take a guided tour, Colour theme (Emerald is the default) and your profile menu.

On a phone the sidebar becomes a bottom bar (Home, Quotes, Leads, Chat, More); everything else is in More.

The top bar

  • Search — press ⌘ K (Mac) or Ctrl K (Windows) on any page. Finds clients, quotes, leads, pages your role can open, Control Center settings, your account items and quick actions like New quote or Invite user.
  • Hide amounts (eye) — hides every amount on screen while you present to a client. Showing them again asks for the studio's 4-digit PIN.
  • Chat — team chat, with a badge for unread messages.
  • Bell — reminders, approvals waiting on you and trial reminders. Click one to jump to it; Mark all read clears the list.
  • New quote — the arrow next to it adds a lead, client, inventory item, vendor, staff member or checklist template (what you see depends on your role).

Your profile menu

Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone, open More). It holds your own account only:

  • Profile — your full name (shown in chat and on assignments), mobile number, job title, department and photo.
  • Account settings — your e-mail, password, two-step verification (a code from an authenticator app) and signing out your other devices. Available to everyone, free trial included.
  • User manual — opens this manual in a new tab.
  • Sign out.

Control Center and Insights are in the sidebar's Admin group; Customize is a button on the dashboard.

Screenshot to addThe profile menu: Profile, Account settings, User manual, Sign out

The profile menu keeps to your own account. Admin pages live in the sidebar.

Guided tours

The dashboard tour starts by itself the first time you sign in, and most pages have their own. Start one any time with Take a guided tour in the sidebar (on a phone: More). The tour highlights each control and explains it; press Esc to leave and the arrow keys to move. While it walks the sidebar, the sidebar opens so you can read every label.

Filling in forms: a red * marks a required field — the rest can be added later. Phone fields accept digits only (with an optional leading +).

5 · Leads, CRM & nurture

Every enquiry starts as a lead. Capture it, move it along as it warms up, then turn it into a quote.

  1. Open Leads and click + New lead: name, phone, e-mail, source, event type, date, guests, budget and notes.
  2. Drag the card across the board (New → Qualified → …) as it progresses, and set a next follow-up date.
  3. When it's ready, click Convert →. Helm creates the quote (= the event) with a code such as 12152026-01 (event date + number) and opens it. Contact details carry over.

The Leads board — drag cards between columns.

  • Saved filters remember views like “Weddings this quarter”.
  • CRM archive keeps every client and enquiry with a full history: quotes, events, payments and messages on one timeline. Archiving or restoring a record adds an entry to its history; nothing is lost.
  • Nurture remembers birthdays, anniversaries and repeat occasions. When one is due it is flagged; Followed up moves it a year ahead and → Lead starts a new enquiry. Ready-made message templates help you write the greeting.

6 · Quotes & the 8-step flow

Every quote is prepared in one guided page with eight steps. You can jump between steps; the right-hand rail shows the live total and the next action.

StepWhat you fill in
1 · Client & eventClient name and phone (required), company, e-mail, event type, date, city, guests and budget. Filled in from the lead.
2 · DiscoveryThe first conversation: meeting date, mode (in person, phone, video), who attended, notes.
3 · RequirementsThe services the client wants, each marked Must-have, Optional or Nice-to-have. These also build the Budget (see Budget & margin).
4 · Venue & accessPick a saved venue or type one: contact, indoor / outdoor, address, access and load-in notes.
5 · Menu packagePick a package (e.g. Gold Veg). It fills in the dishes and drives the catering price. Edit dishes for this event if needed.
6 · ProposalThe creative pitch the client sees: concept, theme and scope highlights.
7 · Floor layoutEnter the hall length × breadth, click Generate default layout, then tweak it in the builder. Chairs and objects feed the price.
8 · QuotationThe live total: chairs, plates, rates, décor / equipment, discount (₹ or %), coupon, tax-exempt client. Save quotation saves a new version.
Screenshot to addThe 8-step quote flow with the live total in the right-hand rail

One page, eight steps. Each step tells you what comes next.

Send, approve, book

  1. After step 8, use Send for approval → in the right rail. It locks that version and gives you the client's approval link to share.
  2. The client approves (see Client approval). The quote becomes Confirmed.
  3. Click Record advance to record the client's advance payment and book the date. The status then reads Confirmed · advance paid.
  • Quotes list tabs: Active · In progress (quotes still being prepared, with who worked on each last) · Quotes · Sent & confirmed · ⚠ Attention · Archive · Deleted.
  • Prices changed? If your rates changed after a quote was priced, a banner offers Review & re-price or Keep current prices.
  • Too many guests for the venue? Before you send, Helm asks you to decide: change venue, reduce guests, extend the space (an overflow tent from inventory or a vendor) or proceed with a note. The decision is kept on the quote.
  • Money is checked by the server. Tax is applied after the discount, and the database recomputes every total, so a quote can never be saved with a wrong total.

Quotes — find any quote by client name or code.

Jobs inside an event

  • Event details / Plan — timings, zones, headcounts; venue and menu, then Lock.
  • Operations (tasks & crew) — add tasks or apply a task template, assign an owner and a due time, set dependencies. Crew get the task in My tasks, accept or decline it, and mark it done with photo evidence; a supervisor verifies it.
  • Resource plan — list what the event needs; Helm checks your own staff and stock first and flags the gaps.
  • Logistics & payments — guests, logistics checklist (apply a checklist template), permits, comms and payment milestones.
  • Run-sheet — the timed schedule for the day: start time, minutes, activity, owner, location.
  • Readiness gate — critical checks (vendors confirmed, stock reserved, team briefed). When everything is green, Mark event ready.
  • Media & gallery — photos and videos; choose which ones the client sees.

9 · Builder 2D/3D & templates

Design the room to real measurements, price every item you place, and show the client a 3D walk-through.

  1. Open the builder from step 7 of the quote or from the event. Enter the hall length & breadth and the guest count (or pick a saved venue) — the canvas scales to your hall with a ruler.
  2. Start from a layout template (your studio's own templates come first), a generated layout (theatre rows, round tables, mixed reception …), a blank floor or a past event.
  3. Drag items from the toolbox (stages, chairs, tables, bars, buffets, lounges, arches, lighting, sound, screens) and search by name. Move, rotate, duplicate; grab an edge handle to stretch a carpet or aisle.
  4. Turn on 📏 Measure (metres or feet) and select an item to see its distance to every wall and its nearest neighbours.
  5. Switch to 3D to present (drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom), use Full screen in client meetings, then Save. The chair count and items flow into the quote's price.

The builder — the price updates as you place items.

Screenshot to addThe same layout in 3D

3D view — true heights and colours for client presentations.

  • Capacity meter — seats placed against the venue's capacity; it flags any block packed tighter than a walkable aisle. If a hall is genuinely too small, Helm tells you how much more space you'd need instead of overpacking.
  • Versions — each save is a layout version linked to the quote version.
  • Layout templates — save a good layout as a studio template (Control Center → Layout templates). Templates fit themselves to each event's hall size and are archived, never deleted.
  • Checklist templates — reusable to-do lists (for example “Wedding logistics”). Create them on the Checklist templates page; apply one from an event's Logistics page and all its tasks drop in.
  • Photo render — turns the plan into a photo-real picture of the room. Renders use paid credits and are limited per month (Control Center → Photo renders); not part of the free trial.

Checklist templates — build once, apply to any event from its Logistics page.

10 · Inventory: reserve, check-out, shortages

Your own stock — chairs, tables, linen, lights, cables — and where it is.

  • Stock items — item, priority (A high value, B mid, C low), category, total owned, committed (reserved for events) and available, unit and unit cost. Add items one by one, from the starter list of common event items, or by Excel / CSV import (not in the trial).
  • Reserve for this event — on an event's inventory page, pick an item and quantity and click Reserve. Reservations are checked against every other event, so two events can never take the same chairs.
  • Check-out / Check-in — issue stock to a staff member or coordinator; when it comes back, record how many returned.
  • Loss report — anything short on return is listed with its value, for sign-off.

When there isn't enough

If you ask for more than is free, Helm says so plainly — for example “Only 20 free, short by 10”. It reserves the 20 and asks how to cover the 10: rent from a vendor (adds a vendor booking to the event), outsource / buy, or leave it for now. Open shortages stay visible on the event workspace until they are covered. Helm never over-commits stock.

11 · Vendors & partners

  • Vendors & partners page — your outside partners: vendors, freelancers, rentals and suppliers, with category, phone, e-mail and the services they offer. Deactivate a partner you no longer use (it is kept, not deleted).
  • Book a partner on an event to cover a gap: which need it covers, cost, advance, status and contract. Booking marks the need as outsourced.
  • Vendor bills & payouts (Settlement) — add each vendor's bill (amount, date, reference) and record payouts against it, in part or in full. Helm refuses a payout above the bill. A wrong bill is voided with a reason, never deleted.
  • Vendors can be added to an event's chat group as contacts (see Chat).

12 · Staff & crew

Plan your in-house team first; reach for vendors and freelancers only for the gaps.

  1. Open Staff → + Add staff: name, role, department, employment (full-time, part-time, on-call), phone, e-mail, day rate, skills and notes.
  2. Choose Save as a record only (no login — you can still assign, call and pay them) or Save and invite to sign in (they get an invitation e-mail and a backup link to create their own Helm login).
  3. Assign staff to event tasks and shifts from the event's Operations page.
  • Schedule next to a person opens the calendar on that person's schedule.
  • Crew on their phones: crew see My tasks and My schedule only — event, date, time, venue and their role. No prices or client contact details.

13 · Venues

Keep the halls and spaces you work in, once, in Control Center → Venues: name, address, usable length × width, seated and standing capacity, access notes and contacts.

  • Pick a venue in step 4 of a quote or in the builder, and the hall size fills in for you.
  • When the guest count is more than the venue holds, Helm asks you to decide before the quote is sent (see Quotes).
  • Venues are never deleted — deactivate one you no longer use.

16 · Event day

Run the day from Event-day command:

  • Arrivals — pull in the team and vendors, then mark Arrived / Left / No-show.
  • Setup checks — stage, sound, lighting: Done or Issue.
  • Guests — count guest groups in.
  • Live stock requests — “3 extension cords”: Issued / Replaced.
  • Live issues — log an issue with severity and owner, then Resolve. Safety incidents have their own tab.

Afterwards, Teardown & returns brings reserved stock back, marks vendors as exited and runs the venue handover checklist.

17 · Budget & margin

Each event has one Budget page that shows what the event costs you against what the client pays.

  • The client's requirements from step 3 of the quote become budget lines, grouped by priority: Estimated cost, Vendor, Quoted to client (filled in from the quote), Actual cost and Margin (amount and %), with totals per group and overall.
  • A Suggested group offers the lines most events need — venue, catering, chairs, décor, sound & lights, staff, logistics, contingency — one click to add.
  • A requirement added later shows as “New requirement: X — add to budget?”. Budget lines are only ever added, never deleted or overwritten by Helm.
  • Change orders — + New change: what changed, the amount charged to the client and the extra cost. When approved, revenue, cost and the client's balance all update.

18 · Settlement & closure

Settlement

  • Client — revenue, payments received and the balance. Record a payment with mode, reference and date. Helm refuses a payment above the confirmed total plus approved changes.
  • Vendors — each booking with cost, advance, outstanding and settled, plus Vendor bills & payouts (see Vendors).
  • Staff expense claims — who, for what, amount → Approve → Mark paid.
  • Refunds & recovery — refund the client, recover a cost (for example broken chairs) or deduct it from a deposit.
Screenshot to addSettlement: client balance, vendors and vendor bills

Settlement — collect from the client, pay your vendors.

Closure

Closure & P&L shows revenue (before tax) minus vendor bills and other costs = net profit and margin %. Tax collected is shown separately; it is not profit. Add the client's rating and feedback, a testimonial (with their consent), lessons learned, and rate vendors and staff.

Closure — profit & loss, feedback, ratings and the Close & archive button.

Helm won't close an event that isn't finished. When you click Close, it checks that the client owes nothing and that no equipment is still checked out. If something is open it lists it and offers to take you to Settlement or Inventory. It then asks you to confirm, and the event moves to the Archive tab.

Across all events: Business shows the studio-wide P&L, what you still owe vendors and cash received by month; Reports exports lists (not in the trial); Insights shows conversion rate, average event value and profit by event type.

  1. Open Control Center → Plan & billing (or Choose a plan from a trial notice).
  2. Pick the plan and monthly / yearly, fill in the billing details shown on your invoices (legal name, address, state, GSTIN optional) and accept the terms.
  3. Pay securely through Razorpay — Helm never sees or stores your card. While online payment is being switched on, the page says so and you can contact sales@helm.events to activate your plan.

Your events, layouts and records stay with your studio when you move between plans.

22 · After the trial

  • Reminders — 3 days before, 1 day before, on the last day, and 7 days after the trial ends (bell, banner and e-mail).
  • Read-only after 8 days — for everyone, including invited teammates. You can sign in and look, but nobody can add, change or delete anything until you choose a plan.
  • Data kept 60 days after the trial ends, then permanently deleted. Helm e-mails you 30, 7 and 1 day before the deletion date.
  • Choose a plan any time — when you upgrade you choose: keep the sample data, remove the sample data and keep your own records, or start completely clean. Later, Control Center offers Delete all sample data.
  • One free trial per person; Helm may approve one extension of up to 7 days.

FAQ

I didn't get the confirmation e-mail

Check spam, then use Resend confirmation link on the sign-in page.

My password is refused

It needs 12+ characters with an uppercase and a lowercase letter, a number and a symbol. Repeated wrong attempts lock the account for a short while.

A teammate can't see something

Their role doesn't have that area. An admin can allow it in Control Center → Roles & access.

Operations staff can see prices

Remove finance from their role in the access matrix.

The total differs from my calculator

Tax is applied after the discount and rounded to whole currency units. The server's total is the correct one.

“Payment exceeds amount due”

Payments can't go above the confirmed total plus approved changes. Add a change order first.

Stock shows a shortage

Another event on the same date has reserved it. Check the calendar, then cover the gap with a vendor.

A client link has expired

Send a fresh link from the event. Link windows are set in Control Center → Client links & packages.

I entered a wrong vendor bill

Void it with a reason and add the correct one.

I'm showing my screen to a client

Click the eye in the top bar to hide every amount; showing them again needs the studio PIN.

Where is Control Center / Insights?

In the sidebar under Admin (on a phone: More). The profile menu only holds your own account.

I created a studio by mistake

Teammates should join through an invite link, not by creating a studio. Ask your admin to invite you.
