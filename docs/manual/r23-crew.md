# Crew: availability, open shifts and event-day check-in

Your crew use the same link they already get for their tasks (the crew link). Three things now live on it.

## 1. Availability (days they can't work)
- **Crew:** open your crew link, scroll to **My availability**, tap a day you can't work (it turns red). Add a reason if you like - only the office sees it. Tap it again when you're free. You can mark up to 120 days ahead.
- **Office (anyone who can edit Staff):** **Staff** page -> **Availability** card. A month grid shows who can't work (red cross) and who is already working (green dot). Tap a day to mark it or clear it.
- **Operations** warns you before you assign someone on a day they marked off, or who already works another event that day.

## 2. Open shifts (crew claim work with one tap)
- **Post (anyone who can edit Staff):** **Operations** -> **Open shifts** -> **Post an open shift**. Give the role (e.g. Waiter), date, start / end time, wage (per day or per hour, optional), how many people, who can take it (anyone, or one department) and how it is given:
  - **First come, first served** - the first people to tap get it; the shift closes itself when full.
  - **Needs my approval** - people ask; you **Approve** or **Decline** on Operations.
- Every eligible crew member sees the shift on their crew link. A WhatsApp message is queued for each of them (it goes out once WhatsApp is switched on for the studio).
- **Crew:** tap **Take this shift** (or **Ask for this shift**). It is added to your tasks as accepted. If you later reject that task, your place opens again for someone else.
- **Office:** **Stop taking claims**, **Re-open** or **Cancel shift** at any time. Cancelling tells people still waiting "no"; people already on it keep their task.

## 3. Event-day check-in and check-out
- **Crew:** on the event day your crew link shows **Check in now**. Tap it when you reach the venue, and **Check out** when you leave. The time is taken from Helm's server, not your phone. Check-in opens 3 hours before your start time.
- If the studio switched on the **location check**, your phone asks for your location and you must be near the venue. Only the distance is saved, never where you are.
- **Office:** **Event day** page -> **Crew check-in & hours** shows start time, in / out times, hours, and flags: **Late** (more than the grace minutes after the start) and **No-show?** (no check-in long after the start). The office is told in the bell when someone checks in late.
- **Enter times / Edit:** people with Event-day or Staff edit rights can enter times for someone who couldn't check in, or correct times. Changing times the crew recorded needs a reason; the crew's own times are kept and shown.
- **Check-in location:** on the same card paste a Google Maps link (or "12.97, 77.59"), or tap **Use my location** at the venue, and set the radius.
- Check-ins also update the **Arrivals & attendance** list (Arrived / Left).

## Wages
On **Budget**, a crew member's **Actual** wage now comes from their check-in:
- **Per day** (default): day rate x days they checked in.
- **Per hour** (Control Center): hours checked in x (day rate / hours in a working day).
- A wage posted on an open shift replaces their usual day rate for that event (per hour shifts are always paid by the hour).
- No check-in: the Arrivals list counts as before (Arrived or Left = one day, No-show = nothing).
Per-person pay is still visible to admins only.

## Control Center -> Crew & shifts (admins / Control Center edit)
Switch each part on or off: availability, open shifts (and the default: first come or needs approval), WhatsApp notices for new shifts, self check-in, location check and its radius, "late after" minutes, "no-show? after" minutes, pay per day or per hour, hours in a working day.
